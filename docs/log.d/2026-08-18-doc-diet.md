## 2026-08-18f — Doc diet: `CLAUDE.md` halved, `docs/status.md` returned to forward-only

**Why.** `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/status.md` are the two highest-frequency agent
reads in this repo, and both had drifted off the kit's own rules. `CLAUDE.md`
spent 55% of its bytes on a file-by-file "Repo map" — derivable from the tree,
and exactly the content Claude-Code guidance says to leave out — plus four
provenance parentheticals that the kit's own "state the constraint, not its
history" rule (`AGENTS.template.md`) forbids. `docs/status.md` opens by
declaring itself **forward-only**, then carried ~11 KB of backward-looking
narrative above `## Current State`.

**What changed.**

- `CLAUDE.md` 9,891 -> 6,677 bytes (-32% overall). The cut is concentrated where
  it was asked for: the "Repo map" section went 5,454 -> 2,236 bytes (-59%),
  collapsing a per-file description of eighteen paths into six entry-point
  pointers; agents read directories. The rest of the file is protected content
  (the framing, the owner-scratchpad prohibition, the principles, the
  communication close), so a whole-file halving was not available without
  deleting rules. Removed: the `(owner ruling 2026-07-28, …)` dependency
  parenthetical, the 272-char `(owner directive 2026-08-14)` ratification
  aside, the `Thread 47` / `Thread 52` labels, the `Amended from … at the
  2026-08-13 sitting` sentence, and the `currently re-opened to DevStg-Reqs`
  gate status (status lives in `docs/status.md`, not in the working-rules file).
  Every **rule** those sentences carried survives, minus its history. Nothing
  was added pointing at `docs/architecture.md`, which is being retired.
- **A wrong number was corrected.** The self-test bullet claimed the smoke tier
  runs `~3.3 min` and the full suite `~4.2 min`. Neither is real: the smoke
  budget is **60 s** wall (`docs/stack.ini` `seconds = 60`), CI-enforced by
  `scripts/check_smoke_budget.py`, and it measured **~17.6 s** on 2026-08-11;
  the full unfiltered suite is **~6 min**. The inflated figure had been quietly
  telling every agent that the per-commit bar costs three minutes it does not
  cost. Canonical wording ("Paste the real output; never report a green you
  didn't produce.") is untouched.
- `docs/status.md` 16,202 -> 10,289 bytes, 230 -> 144 lines. The `check_docs`
  S-1 line-budget warning (budget 120) is much reduced but **not cleared** —
  110 lines over became 24, and closing the rest would mean relocating the
  "Standing rules with no other home" block, which has no home to go to yet.
  The backward-looking narrative is reproduced verbatim below. Kept
  in status.md: the forward-only charter, the sitting target and its two
  reading documents, the one still-open call, the `wi455-architecture-retirement`
  lane, standing owner acts, the STARTING-COLD order, the LOCKED depth-0 frame,
  unfiled follow-ups, the generated `## Current State` block, the commit bar,
  the standing rules with no other home, and `## Scope`.
- One warn-only fix taken while in the file: `check_docs` S-2 wanted an
  "Open items" marker above `## Scope`, and the header's `open-items.html`
  pointer now carries the phrase.

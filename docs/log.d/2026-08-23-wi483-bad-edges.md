## 2026-08-23 — WI-483 slice 3: the two documented bad edges, and the facade left with no importers

**Summary.** Item 2 of WI-483's STILL OWED list. The 2026-08-19 review recorded
two bad edges that were never inside the SCC and so were never on slices 1–2's
critical path: `dispatch._pending_cards` reaching into `gen_trajectory` for the
PRIVATE `_blocked_pending`/`_spine_pending` (M-02, and the crossing was
DOCUMENTED as `IF-088` rather than removed), and `gen_open_items` importing the
same ~1,000-line facade for a state query (H-02). Both are gone. The pending
projection now lives in a new sibling `project-trajectory/scripts/pending.py`,
below all three of its readers, and `gen_trajectory` — a CLI entry point that
re-exports the `traj_*` family — is imported by **nothing**, which is asserted
rather than described. Items 1, 3 and 4 stay owed; nothing here touched them.

### The per-edge census, read before either cut was designed

Both edges pointed at the same facade, and both were reaching THROUGH it at code
that lives in `traj_status.py`:

- **`dispatch -> gen_trajectory`** (deferred, one call site behind
  `_pending_cards`, two readers of that). What crossed was two PRIVATE
  functions, `_blocked_pending` and `_spine_pending`, re-assembled at the
  dispatcher into "the cards, minus the pause". Both of its consumers then took
  `len()` of the result: the composer wanted a COUNT, and to get it, it imported
  a render family and named two underscored functions in another module.
  `IF-088`'s own Contract cell spelled the arrangement out — "imports the
  gen_trajectory FACADE … and reuses `_blocked_pending` + `_spine_pending`" —
  which is a seam registry recording a crossing rather than licensing it.
- **`gen_open_items -> gen_trajectory`** (module-level, one call site). What
  crossed was `pending_block(root)`, the whole rendered pending region, reused
  verbatim so the owner surface never grows a second opinion about what is
  pending. The REUSE was right and is unchanged; the route was wrong — a view
  asking another view's facade for state.

Those two were the facade's ONLY importers, which is why cutting them was worth
one module: `gen_trajectory` is now an entry point and nothing else.

### The cut

`pending.py` — the sibling of `census.py`, answering the other half of the same
question. Where the census asks *which gaps do the registries name?*, this asks
*which durable actions does the OWNER hold?*: `blocked_pending` (queued rows with
a `blockref`), `spine_pending` (`Drafted` SRs and SRs drifted from
`last_approved`), `pause_pending` (the tracked `docs/work/pause`), all moved
verbatim out of `traj_status.py`. Above them, the two things this slice added:

- `pending_items(root)` — the TYPED read model, one `PendingItem(kind, line)`
  per action. `kind` is a field, so the one caller that discriminates is not
  parsing prose to make a control-flow decision.
- `owner_cards(root)` — `pending_items` minus the pause, declared ONCE here
  rather than re-assembled at the banner from two of the three sources. The
  WI-381 amendment (ruled 2026-08-01) requires that the drained-queue banner and
  `open-items.html` can never disagree about what is blocking; one read model
  with one declared filter is that requirement held by construction instead of
  by three callers agreeing to be careful.

`pending_block` renders the model and is byte-identical to what it replaced —
`gen_open_items.py --check` and `gen_trajectory.py --status --check` both report
up to date against the committed artifacts, unchanged. `traj_status.py`
re-exports all four under their former names (`_blocked_pending`,
`_spine_pending`, `_pause_pending`, `pending_block`, plus `PAUSE_MALFORMED`), so
the facade's re-export list, the dashboard and every existing test are
untouched; `dispatch._pending_cards` is now `pending.owner_cards(root)` and
`gen_open_items.pending_block_text` is `pending.pending_block(root)`.

**Why a plain sibling and not `kitlib`,** for the third time and the same hard
reason: every module of that package must stay import-clean of the rest of
`scripts/` (`tests/test_bootstrap.py::test_bootstrap_imports_only_the_common_package`)
because `bootstrap.py` imports it, and this module reads the work registry
through the validator's loaders and the spine through `traj_parse`.

  doesn't require the operator to read it, it just acts as a ledger for the
  operator to review if the operator believes there might be risk in some
  assumptions made." And on a run's honesty: the unattended mode presumes an
  agent records honestly, a premise that is falsifiable if vetted.

  misdescribed call falsifies it. Standing stays `active`. Argon (low)

  fields named, a holding condition in place of the ruling, the verdict
  clause left to SR-225). The re-accept is the next commit.

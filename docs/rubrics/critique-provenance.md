# Rubric — critique provenance (TC-209)

**Adjudicates:** SR-184. **Used by:** TC-209.

**Intent:** Acceptance is auditable against independently derived intent, with reviewer identity and fixed anchor citations.

Pass requires every R anchor and no B pattern. Record the rubric revision and cite anchor IDs. The TC and [inspection procedures](../test/inspection-procedures.md) supply the how.

## Anchors

**R1 — The complete record identifies a fresh non-author reviewer/session, rubric revision, applicable SN/SR intent basis, and numbered anchors for every verdict and finding.**

**R2 — The rubric is independently derived from SN/SR intent; a populated record that merely copies its verifying TC is rejected.**

**R3 — Every omitted reviewer, rubric, intent, or anchor fact in an abnormal record is reported as a finding.**

**B1 — Missing provenance or TC-only rubric derivation is accepted.**

**B2 — Process inspection is substituted for the separate Critique judgement of artifact quality.**

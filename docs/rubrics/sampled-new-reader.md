# Rubric — sampled new reader (TC-279)

**Adjudicates:** DA-011. **Used by:** TC-279.

**Intent:** A sampled reader can explain both behavior and purpose from the code and its linked records, bounding what the sample establishes.

Pass requires every R anchor and no B pattern. Record the rubric revision and cite anchor IDs. The TC and [inspection procedures](../test/inspection-procedures.md) supply the how.

## Anchors

**R1 — Every randomly drawn part is read by a person or fresh model session that did not author it, with the reader kind and each statement recorded beside the linked requirement/design rows.**

**R2 — All five readers state what their part does and why it exists without asking its author; each statement agrees with the linked rows.**

**B1 — Any reader cannot explain behavior or purpose, or contradicts the linked rows: the sample fails and falsifies DA-011.**

**B2 — An author-selected sample or a claim of assurance for unsampled parts is accepted.**

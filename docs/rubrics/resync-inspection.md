# Rubric — resync inspection (TC-036)

**Adjudicates:** SR-036. **Used by:** TC-036.

**Intent:** A re-sync preserves the adopter’s authored project while taking the kit-owned update completely.

Pass requires every R anchor and no B pattern. Record the rubric revision and cite anchor IDs. The TC and [ADOPTING.md §6](../../project-trajectory/ADOPTING.md) supply the how.

## Anchors

**R1 — Kit-owned files match the selected kit revision wholesale; the kit-version diff identifies the update.**

**R2 — Filled-in project files retain the adopter’s content and policy choices.**

**R3 — Generated documents are regenerated from their inputs after the update.**

**B1 — Partial kit copying or overwritten filled-in content fails.**

**B2 — Stale generated documents or an unexplained version diff fails.**

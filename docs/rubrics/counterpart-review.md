# Rubric — counterpart review (TC-210)

**Adjudicates:** SR-185. **Used by:** TC-210.

**Intent:** A requirement or interface change preserves the meaning of its affected counterpart through an independent semantic decision.

Pass requires every R anchor and no B pattern. Record the rubric revision and cite anchor IDs. The TC and [inspection procedures](../test/inspection-procedures.md) supply the how.

## Anchors

**R1 — The change record identifies the changed side and affected counterpart, and supplies the corresponding change or an explicit justification for retaining it.**

**R2 — An independent semantic decision is recorded; the reference-existence-only abnormal record is found insufficient.**

**B1 — Resolved IDs, endpoints, or references are accepted as semantic agreement by themselves.**

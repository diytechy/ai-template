# Rubric — decomposition proportionality (TC-211)

**Adjudicates:** SR-161, SR-186. **Used by:** TC-211.

**Intent:** Within the required spine, further decomposition earns its place through an independent decision or verification purpose.

Pass requires every R anchor and no B pattern. Record the rubric revision and cite anchor IDs. The TC and [inspection procedures](../test/inspection-procedures.md) supply the how.

## Anchors

**R1 — The sample is one actual completed decomposition review record whose brief presents every applicable hat's question and whose record names each applicable hat's outcome, within the required spine boundary.**

**R2 — Each retained child has an independent decision or verification purpose, and the record explains why further splitting stops.**

**R3 — The extra paraphrasing child with no independent purpose is reported as a finding.**

**B1 — An applicable hat's outcome is missing from the review record, or prose or Hat-Refs is presented in place of the review record's outcomes.**

**B2 — A paraphrasing child is accepted merely to add depth or satisfy a row-count target.**

## Traps this work paid for

- **A tick is not a completion.** `S5` was marked done on a gating signal
  (`form-findings` hit 0) rather than against its own checklist, and **47 cells
  reached the window unreviewed**. The checklist is the contract; the green is
  not.
- **A guard that cannot fail is not a guard.** Every new check here was
  mutation-proven — the rule was removed and the test was confirmed to fail.
- **`\betc\.\b` can never match.** A word boundary after a period only exists
  mid-sentence. The measurement that "proved" the vague-term rule fired on zero
  rows used the same broken pattern; a test's *positive* half caught it.
- **Requirement-text work sequences INTO an open window, not after it.** Now a
  standing rule in `PROCESS.md` §4 — the owner's call, and it is why this is one
  sitting instead of three.

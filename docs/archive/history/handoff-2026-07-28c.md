## 5. The train discussion (owner challenge → revised recommendation)

The owner pushed back on retiring the train machinery: significant work made
it runnable, and the expectation is heavy downstream use for parallelizing
work. The honest both-sides record:

**For keeping:** most historical failures were bootstrapping defects that were
fixed when hit (WI-282 trailer, WI-238/239/240 blocked-exit, WI-286
interpreter); today's machinery is better than its record. Its worst
structural failure — the render-train deadlock — was the critique gate's
fault, which layer #3 retires anyway. And three of the eight clean autonomous
landings (WI-283/285/286) were the machinery fixing itself unattended
overnight — the exact capability downstream would want, demonstrated.

**Against advertising it as supported today:** its core promise — *gated*
parallel integration — has **never once executed** (integration bar skipped
on every autonomous integration ever). And there is a design-level ceiling,
not just a bug backlog: every train must touch the shared spine files
(registry, dashboard, census, ratchet stamps) to close its WI, so parallel
trains contend on the same generated artifacts **by construction** — that is
what forced the 2026-07-24 hand-integrations, and WI-289 (auto-resolve at
compose) is still open. Downstream inherits that contention exactly.

**Revised recommendation — mothball, don't retire:**

1. Keep the code; retire the *obligation*. Opt-in via bootstrap (#1), marked
   **experimental** in PROCESS_OPTIONS.md with an honest applies-when; freeze
   feature investment here until a demand signal. WI-343 survives only in its
   narrowed correctness form.
2. Define the proof before it is advertised as supported: one end-to-end
   acceptance run on a scaffolded sandbox repo — 2–3 independent WIs, full
   autonomous cycle, the integration bar **demonstrably running**
   (`integration-bar: pass` in the journal, not `skipped`), zero hand-rescues.
   An afternoon to attempt; converts "would be heavily used downstream" from
   an unmeasured claim into a measured one.
3. Sequence that proof **after** the critique-gate flip (#3), so a failure
   means something about the dispatcher rather than about SR-054.
4. Clean the residue now regardless: 36 worktrees, 34 `llm/*` branches, the
   stale `RUNNING` run-state, the orphaned stash (diff any deleted branch for
   orphaned *files* first — the 2026-07-26 lesson).

## Why

Eleven SR rows exceed 2,000 characters — notably SR-044, SR-045, SR-060,
SR-061, SR-065, SR-066 (~3,800 chars) — each combining routing, scoring,
fallbacks, escalation, provenance, safety behavior, and acceptance criteria
in one "atomic" ID. They are traceable but not independently reviewable or
changeable: a small policy amendment forces a large requirement and all its
linked evidence to re-ratify. The review's constraint is binding: **no
mechanical sentence split, no renumbering of existing IDs in a cleanup
commit** — this is a planned migration with supersession and evidence
preservation, or it is not done at all.

## Scope (staged; the spine changes land only in stage 3)

1. **Migration plan (docs only).** For each oversized SR: enumerate its
   distinct obligations; propose the split into stable NEW SR IDs (existing
   IDs are never renumbered — a split parent becomes a supersession link, or
   survives as the umbrella with narrowed text); map every existing LLR/TC
   to its post-split parent; state the SN coverage after the split. One
   reviewable plan document, cross-checked by `trace.py --strict-integrity`
   dry-run reasoning, not vibes. *(If the owner prefers rival plans, this
   stage is the natural PlanMode=dual candidate — the registry would need
   the `PlanMode` column added; not assumed here.)*
2. **Ratification page — hard stop.** Splitting Verified/attested rows
   re-opens their ratification. The build session MUST NOT self-ratify:
   after stage 1 it marks this WI **blocked** with BlockRef to the plan doc
   and the run-state ask naming the attestation needed (gate-advance skill:
   attestation is the human's move, recorded with Attest). Autonomous
   gate-policy does not waive this — the stop is declared here, in the spec
   the worker executes.
3. **Execute the ratified plan.** New SR/LLR/TC rows minted with
   supersession links per the plan; amended rows re-verified; `derive_gate`
   consequences accepted honestly (a temporarily regressed artifact state is
   reported, never masked); OKF/dashboard/status regenerate; EXAMPLE.md and
   any doc citing a split SR id updated. Every open WI citing an amended SR
   gets the staleness warning cleared by explicit re-affirmation.

**Non-goals:** no LLR/TC prose rewrites beyond re-parenting and honest
narrowing; no gate advancement/regression decisions inside this WI beyond
what the derived gate computes; no renumbering; no PROCESS.md changes.

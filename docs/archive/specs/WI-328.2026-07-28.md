# WI-328 — A requirement carries its own reason

**Status: DONE** — closed 2026-07-28. The six substantive items landed 2026-07-27;
the row itself was left on `queued` until the owner asked whether this was not
already complete. Nothing reconciled these boxes with the registry row — WI-352.

## The observation

WI-321 and WI-327 banned **provenance** in normative spine text — a `WI-###` id
or a citation of the process doc the row obeys. The ban is right and it is
`29148`-aligned. But it removed the *pointer* without requiring the *substance*,
and that is why it has not terminated:

- 124-REVIEW-A, given only the claim *"only provenance was removed"*, found 23
  more survivors of the same kind wearing different costumes — `Thread 53`,
  `119-CRITIQUE`, `the 2026-07-26 owner ruling`, `REWORKED same day after
  adversarial review`. There will always be another costume, because provenance
  is not a token shape.
- The 48-cell rewrite that dropped the ids left dangling participles behind
  (*"and delivered: every element…"*) — the tell that removing a citation left a
  hole where the reason used to be.

The owner's framing names the real defect: a rationale that cites a historical
artifact **without bubbling up the impact** makes the reader go find and extract
the justification. Three things are being conflated in one cell — the **what**,
the **why summarized with its impact and its ruled-out alternatives**, and the
**why decomposed with the evidence that led there**. Only the third belongs
outward, as an optional pointer.

**The terminating rule is a positive obligation, not a wider prohibition.**

## Measured before filing (2026-07-27, all three spine registries)

The scoping error WI-327 recorded about itself — *"I measured only the registry
the example came from"* — so every candidate below is measured across **SR + LLR
+ TC**, and the counts decide which tier it lands in.

Five of the six gating checks fire on **0–1 rows**. They are guards, not
cleanups — which is the shape WI-327's ruling asked for (*"the rule guards
zero-to-zero rather than dictating a cleanup"*). Only C1 carries a real
population, and 13 rows is a single sitting's work.

C8's 38 hits are exactly why it must **not** gate: a short `Detail` legitimately
shares vocabulary with its parent. It belongs in the advisory pipe beside
`ac_advisories`, where a heuristic is labelled as one.

### The rationale measurement, and the check it refutes

| Fact | Number |
|---|---|
| SR `Rationale` length | min 45, median **104**, max 545 |
| SR rationales under 120 chars | **75** / 110 |
| SR rationales carrying a history citation | **22** / 110 (12 already `Modified`, 10 `Verified`) |
| SR rationales that are empty | **0** |

The obvious check — a `Rationale` length floor — is **wrong, and the measurement
is what shows it.** SR-016's rationale is 45 characters (*"Realizes SN-008 — a
green never hides a stub"*) and is **complete**: it states the impact if the
requirement is absent. Length is not the defect.

The defect is the **22 rows where a citation carries the reason instead of the
prose**. Those, and only those, are the rewrite population.

### Where the provenance actually still is

| Layer | Rows with history-shaped text | already `Modified` | `Verified` (owes a fresh re-attest) |
|---|---|---|---|
| SR | 22 / 110 — **all in `Rationale`; `Requirement` is clean across all 110** | 12 | 10 |
| LLR | 16 / 118 — all in `Detail` | 15 | 1 |
| TC | 1 / 121 | 1 | 0 |

Two conclusions follow, and they redirect the WI:

1. **The SR layer's *what* is already fixed.** Zero `Requirement` cells carry
   provenance. This is a `Rationale`-quality WI, not a requirements-text WI.
2. **The LLR has nowhere to put a why.** SR has `Requirement` / `Rationale` /
   `AcceptanceCriteria`; LLR has one prose cell, `Detail`. The what, the why, the
   ruled-out alternatives, the scope limits and the authoring chronology are
   *structurally* forced into one field. `LLR-112` is the exhibit — 2,232
   characters holding a normative statement, a derivation mechanism, a narrowing
   ruling, a two-claim changelog of an adversarial review, and three limits.

That is a **schema** defect, not an authoring one, and it is why the fat cells
are recent: 75 of 118 `Detail`s are under 300 characters. The 24 walls are
`LLR-099`…`LLR-120`, the render/critique cluster written in the last fortnight —
the rows whose reasons were richest and had nowhere to go.

## Scope

### S1 — Correct `PROCESS.md` §3 *(no registry touch; lands in its own commit, first)*

The shipped description of the stand-alone rule contradicts the shipped checker
in two places. WI-327 widened and gated the rule and did not touch `PROCESS.md`:

| §3 says today | The checker does |
|---|---|
| *"inside an `SR`'s normative text"* | every normative cell of **all three** spine registries |
| *"`trace.py` warns (never gates)"* | **gates** under `--strict`, via `exit_code` |

A downstream adopter currently inherits a rule description that is wrong about
its own scope and its own severity. This step has no registry touch and no
attestation cost — it must not wait behind the schema change.

Re-stamp the byte baseline in all three skill copies (`byte-budget-guard`).

### S2 — The requirement-form standard: stated in `PROCESS.md`, checked in `trace.py`

Three tiers, because the measurement gives three populations:

- **Gating** (`--strict`, via `exit_code`, beside `provenance_findings`): C1–C6.
  Deterministic, no judgement, and the whole population is cleanable in S5 so
  each lands guarding zero-to-zero.
- **Advisory** (its own pipe + counter, warn-only forever, the `ac_advisories`
  precedent): C7, C8. These are heuristics and must be labelled as heuristics —
  a rule that cries wolf is a rule that gets scrolled past.
- **Ratchet** (`tests/test_module_size_ratchet.py` idiom — an absolute recorded
  maximum that may fall and never rise): C9, C10. Set from the post-S5 numbers,
  not today's.

Each check ships with the **negative half** in its test, as WI-321/327 did: a
legitimate multi-clause `AcceptanceCriteria`, a script name, an artifact path
and a rubric reference must not trip anything.

### S3 — Add a `Rationale` column to the LLR registry

`LLR-ID,SR-Refs,Title,Module,CodeSymbol,Detail,Rationale,TestRefs,Status,Component,Phase`.

`Rationale`, not `History`: provenance already has two homes
([`work-items.csv`](../../work/) and [`log.md`](../../log.md))
and a third would be the duplication this kit exists to prevent. Rationale is an
attribute of a requirement **at every level** in `29148`, which is the reason the
SR has one and the asymmetry was the bug.

Blast radius — the honest list, not the optimistic one:
`registries/low-level-requirements.template.csv` · `trace.py` · `gen_okf.py` ·
`derive_gate.py` · `gen_trajectory.py` · `bootstrap.py` · `EXAMPLE.md` ·
`PROCESS.md` · `ADOPTING.md` (a §6 re-sync migration note) ·
`tests/test_dogfood_sync.py` · `tests/conftest.py` · the three
`tests/golden/*.txt`. **This is a downstream migration event** — an adopter
re-syncing gets a new column — and `ADOPTING.md` must say so.

### Interface boundaries (§8 seams)

**No new seam.** [`IF-021`](../../requirements/interfaces.toml) already contracts
*"reads the SN→SR→LLR→TC spine … and joins them by id"*; an added column extends
that contract rather than creating one. The nearest alternatives fall short for
the same reason — `IF-033` (`gen_okf`) and `IF-051` (`derive_gate`) are the same
registry read from different consumers, not a different seam. S3 bumps
`IF-021` / `IF-033` / `IF-051` from `v1` to `v2`; the addition is additive, so
`Stability=Stable` holds.

### S4 — The rationale content contract

Stated once in `PROCESS.md` §3, beside the stand-alone rule it completes:

> A rationale is complete when a reader with none of this project's history can
> read the row and know **what breaks if the requirement is absent**, and **which
> alternative was considered and why it lost** where one was. A reference to a
> review, a ruling or a design thread is then legitimate *and optional* — a
> further-context pointer appended to a self-contained sentence, never a
> substitute for one.

That framing is what makes the rule terminate: the 23 costumes 124-REVIEW-A
found stop being violations the moment the sentence in front of them stands on
its own, and the ones that still say nothing get rewritten rather than hunted.

### S5 — The single registry pass *(one commit)*

Everything S2–S4 flags, in **one** spine touch, because each separate pass flips
`Verified` rows to `Modified` and owes a fresh attestation:

- the 13 multi-`shall` SRs split or reworded (C1), plus the four one-off lint
  hits (C2/C3/C5);
- the 22 SR rationales where a citation is carrying the reason — impact and
  ruled-out alternative brought into the prose, the citation kept only where it
  adds context beyond it;
- the 24 fat LLR `Detail`s decomposed into `Detail` (mechanism) + `Rationale`
  (why), and the 16 carrying history-shaped text;
- `TC-120`.

Cost: **~11 rows newly `Modified`**, joining a sitting that already holds 50.
The alternative — doing this after the sitting — is a *second* sitting of ~39.

### S6 — Adversarial review of the rewritten cells

Not optional. WI-327's 48-cell rewrite needed 124-REVIEW-A, which returned 30
findings of which **7 were real defects including two BLOCKERs**, and the
editor's own word-level second pass then found **two more the critic missed**
(*"the independent critic was necessary and not sufficient"*). This pass is
larger. Budget one independent review **and** one word-level delta run, and do
not run S5 unattended.

## Deliberately NOT in scope

- **A wider provenance blocklist** — the WI's original framing. Chasing costumes
  does not terminate; S4's positive obligation does. `provenance_findings` keeps
  exactly the two token shapes it has.
- **Implementation-leakage detection in an SR.** This kit's *product* IS its
  scripts, so naming one is the subject under specification, not the *how* —
  the same negative half `provenance_findings` already depends on.
- **A readability score or a `Rationale` length floor.** WI-321 refused proxy
  metrics and the measurement above refutes the floor specifically: a 45-char
  rationale can be complete. C9/C10 are absolute counts, not scores.
- **Enriching the ~75 short-but-complete rationales.** The 22 citation-carrying
  rows are the measured defect; the rest are narrow by measurement.
- **A `History` / `Provenance` column.** See S3.
- **WI-324's de-triplication** — a different fact, and `TC-055` is already
  `Modified`, so it costs nothing either way.

## Sequencing — **RULED 2026-07-27: run inside the open window**

WI-328's original filing said *"do NOT run this inside an open attestation
window"*, on the assumption the window was closing. It is not: 50 rows are
`Modified` and unblessed, and 28 of the 39 rows this WI rewrites are already
among them. Landing first folds ~11 new rows into one sitting; landing after
creates a second sitting of ~39.

The owner ruled it **inside** the window, and generalized it: *"work items that
affect requirement documentation should always be performed with preference
against attesting artifacts or else it means more work."* That is a standing
rule, so it lives in [`PROCESS.md`](../../../project-trajectory/PROCESS.md) §4
beside the `Modified` definition — not here, and not only in the log.

The general preference against touching attested artifacts still holds. This WI
is the exception the rule names: the work **is** the requirement documentation,
so deferring it past the sitting does not protect the attestation, it duplicates
it.

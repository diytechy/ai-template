# WI-373 — Symmetric skepticism: findings are claims; deletion takes evidence; rearchitecture is sanctioned

**WI:** WI-373 · **Status:** queued · **Delivers:** process-doc hardening (no spine change)

## Problem

Skepticism in the review protocol runs one direction. The reviewer charter
(process-options.md "Unattended operation") makes the reviewer treat the
implementation report as claims and re-run everything — but nothing charters
the *finding's owner* to hold a review finding to the same bar before acting
on it. The review-round era measured the cost (the 2026-07-28 audit; account
in [log.md](../../log.md)): rounds 127→131 produced findings that were ~70%
claims/hollow-guards/bookkeeping, re-review rounds 128–129 aimed 100% at the
prior round's fixes and converged to zero real findings while manufacturing
work, and two refuted-in-opposite-directions CPU-cap claims are pinned in
[status.md](../../status.md) as the standing exhibit. The same one-way trust let
previously-parked work be scrubbed by sessions that never read the record
justifying it, and left no sanctioned shape for "this design is wrong — the
right move is costly rework" (owner directive 2026-07-30, filed this WI).

## Approach

Documentation-only; three homes, each fact stated once:

1. **`project-trajectory/PROCESS_OPTIONS.md` — "The LLM-gate verdict
   protocol"** gains the finding lifecycle: a finding names a concrete,
   falsifiable failure scenario; its owner **confirms** (reproduces) or
   **refutes** it before changing code; a refutation is a legitimate recorded
   outcome (round record, with evidence), feeding the reviewer's
   confirmed-finding rate, not the builder's standing. A **re-review round
   verifies the fixes landed — it does not hunt fresh findings in them**;
   fresh finding-hunts aim at product surfaces.
2. **`project-trajectory/AGENTS.template.md` — "Working agreement"** names
   the two missing judgment rules, tightly: **reversal evidence** (deleting
   or reverting takes the same evidence as creating — read the record that
   justified the thing first) and the **symmetric sunk-cost failure**
   (sunk-cost *keeping* / moth-balling: a design found wrong escalates as a
   written case — what was found, why the shape can't reach the requirement,
   cost of both paths — to its owner as a design-change item; it never parks
   unrecorded and is never silently reverted). Paid for inside the
   10,000-byte budget per the file's own Customizing rule
   (`byte-budget-guard` before/after).
3. **`docs/enforcement-audit.md`** records the new rules at their honest
   tiers (Reviewer/Prose) with stated reasons, per the audit's own bar.

**No new checks, scripts, or gates** — the 2026-07-28 audit's warning against
enforcement-layer growth applies; the judgment rules are honestly Prose
(enforcement-audit finding 3).

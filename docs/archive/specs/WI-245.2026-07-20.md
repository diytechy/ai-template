## Why

Downstream adopters may want domain expertise (UI/UX, marketing, legal, finance)
or workflow accelerators that already exist as third-party agent-skill packs —
but the field is noisy (the 2026 "skills"-virality wave inflates star counts),
licenses vary widely (MIT / Apache-2.0 / CC BY / source-available / **none**),
and nearly every pack ships `npx`/installer/live-`--llm` tooling that violates
the kit's **stdlib-only / no-install** stance. A **curated reference index** —
pointers + trust/license/applicability metadata + explicit "mine, don't install"
governance — gives adopters a vetted starting point **without the kit vendoring
or endorsing third-party code**. It is a **reference doc** (like
[EXAMPLE.md](../../../project-trajectory/EXAMPLE.md) /
[ADOPTING.md](../../../project-trajectory/ADOPTING.md) /
[MULTI_REPO.md](../../../project-trajectory/MULTI_REPO.md)), **not scaffolded**, and
**distinct from** the kit's first-party `project-trajectory/skills/`.

- **No scaffolding** — reference doc, like EXAMPLE.md (no `bootstrap.py`
  MAPPING entry; downstream adopters read it in `project-trajectory/`).
- **No endorsement/SLA** — pointers with caveats, not a blessed dependency list.

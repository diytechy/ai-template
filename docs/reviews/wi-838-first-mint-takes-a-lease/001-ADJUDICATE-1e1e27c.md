- [MEANING] LLR-270 Detail -> before: a route with no record mints with no lease taken before launch (the lease lives on an existing record only); a retired or absent record mints; a raising launch's keep_abandon retires the resumed session; a tombstone always names a session to retire; and, GENERALLY, keep_for waits up to lease_wait on ANY lease another call holds and then runs unretained, and keep_bookkeep and keep_abandon RELEASE the lease -> after: where no record exists keep_for writes a lease-only record {family, route_id, lease} under the store lock BEFORE launch; a lease-only record mints and only an active or draining record resumes; a mint lands only while the route record still carries its own lease, else it lands on nothing with reset reason `store moved on` (whether the taker still runs or has finished); a raising first mint removes its lease-only record; a tombstone may remove a lease-only record; an expired lease retires a crashed first call's lease-only record; BUT the wait clause now reads "a second call meeting THAT lease" (the first-mint lease only) and the sentence "keep_bookkeep and keep_abandon release it" is gone -> not the same: a correct implementation of the old text (first mint holds no lease; the later bookkeeping replaces the earlier session's record) FAILS the new text (lease-only record before launch; `store moved on`; lease-only removal on raise). The amendment also DROPPED two general obligations the old text imposed and the code still keeps (`session_keep.keep_for` waits on any held lease; `keep_bookkeep` pops the lease at :798, and `keep_abandon` releases it): under the new wording, an implementation that never releases a resumed session's lease, or never waits on a lease held on an active record, is no longer contradicted by the text.

Not blessed as written. The added first-mint obligations are sound and match WI-838's Done-when. The dropped general wait and release clauses are a lost obligation, so the row is returned through `## Dispositions` in WI-838's spec. It is not re-attested.

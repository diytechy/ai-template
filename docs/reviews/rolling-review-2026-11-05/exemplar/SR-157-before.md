A comma does not end a clause: it cuts the clause into segments, and the segments into lower-cased words joined by single hyphens, so a hyphenated compound is one word and names a mode. A universal quantifier in UNIVERSAL_TERMS takes the next DOMAIN_WINDOW words (6) of its own segment as its domain, while the idiom at all is emphasis rather than a quantifier. A negative in NEGATIVE_TERMS counts only as the first word of the clause's first segment, so one inside a clause or after a comma is not read, and not at all when it precedes a comparative in BOUND_WORDS. A temporal absolute in TEMPORAL_TERMS takes its whole clause across its commas, whose subject is what it ranges over. An absolute whose domain holds a CLOSED_DOMAIN_WORDS word (a registry, its rows and cells, the spine and off-spine row nouns, an id, a declared set), an adjacent CLOSED_DOMAIN_PAIRS pair (test case, work item) or a spine-id-shaped token is suppressed; the rest are reported with the words that follow them. The advisories come one per row and scanned cell, naming the tier, id and cell, the terms with their context, and the reason cell a waiver belongs in; absolute_summary gives the console one line counting them per tier while absolute_report_lines renders the report's own section listing them, or a line saying there are none, and neither joins a failure set.
rationale (963 chars): The rule asks whether a row names the domain its absolute ranges over, not whether that domain is truly closed: meaning is the reviewer's to judge, and a lexical rule that tried would put standing false accusations in the report. Reading the domain from a short word list and a fixed window keeps the tokenization reproducible by hand from its documentation, which a stemmer or a parser would not. A negative inside a clause describes one case's input or outcome rather than a universal, and counting it raised the requirement tier's negative findings from 14 to 154 on the registry it was measured against, the additions all case descriptions. The console carries a count and the report the list, because a registry written before the rule yields hundreds of rows that would bury every other warning. A test case is out because it states a method rather than an obligation and has no reason cell to hold a waiver; adding one would be a schema change for no gain.

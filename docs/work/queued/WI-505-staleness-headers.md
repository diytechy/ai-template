## Context

Executes OI-56 as RULED — (a) only, with two owner-set narrowings:
- **No live-surface pointer in archive headers** ("that will create
  backlink effort that will very quickly rot"). The archive header says
  history-not-guidance and STOPS: "ARCHIVE — design history as of <date>;
  not current guidance." Nothing else.
- **No enforcement check** — the (c) half is declined; the stamp is a
  convention plus the archive README's one-line statement of it.

The generated headers keep the ruling's two forms, stamped BY THE
GENERATORS (so they cannot rot):
- generated-but-LIVE (docs/stage, the status block, open-items.html):
  "GENERATED by <script> — do not hand-edit; cite <source-of-truth>, not
  this rendering; regenerate: <cmd>."
- generated REPORTS (test report, dashboards): the fuller "may be stale
  relative to the tree — regenerate before trusting" form.
The staged-divergence step's 9 declared artifact paths are the generated
population. Archive stamping runs AFTER WI-504's relocation (needs) so
moved history is stamped once, in place. One-time banner sweep over
docs/archive/**; templates shipped to adopters gain the same generator
stamps; RESYNC entry.

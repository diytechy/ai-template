Advisory-in-machinery plus imperative-in-prose is a contradiction, and the prose is the half an LLM obeys. 'Review records of precedent rows' (intake.py:259) carries predecessor and cancelled-row VERDICTS with the same property. THE COUNTERWEIGHT, WHICH MUST NOT BE DISCARDED: this section exists for a MEASURED reason - it prevents re-proposing the refuted, which is the WI-391 failure mode, and WI-391 is this repo's only park and it WAS a mis-assumption (docs/rulings-context-2026-08-01.md §R3). Softening the tone into a regression would be worse than the anchoring. THE ASK, three judgements: (1) can a joined fact be stated as a FACT ('WI-391 was cancelled; reason: ...') leaving the inference to the reader, WITHOUT losing the WI-391 protection - i.e. is the imperative doing work the fact alone would not do, and is that demonstrable rather than assumed; (2) should a clipped free-prose Deliverable be quoted as 'reason' at all, given the 160-char mid-word truncation reproduces the exact WI-416 anchoring shape; (3) does any OTHER derived-prose surface reaching a claim state a CONCLUSION where it could state an OBSERVATION - worker_prompt's pred_block deliverables (clipped 200), the REWORK FINDING block (clipped 80), the minted row's ## Context. Scope: intake.context_block, its section headers and formatters, and tests; agent_loop.worker_prompt only if judgement (3) finds a second site. NOT a licence to weaken the joins: the joins are good and their content is load-bearing - the question is only whether their PROSE prescribes where it could inform."

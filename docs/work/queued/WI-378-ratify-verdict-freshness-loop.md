Decide the shape deliberately: (a) leave it and DOCUMENT the required ordering (close + ratify BEFORE the final verdict round) in process-options.md's verdict protocol and the session-protocol skill; (b) widen the freshness exclusion to the bookkeeping paths that cannot change product behavior (docs/work/, docs/ratify/, docs/gate) - narrower than it sounds, since those are exactly the paths the RULING-6 audit already treats as bookkeeping; or (c) have the reviewer record a forward-looking ratification the flip commit cites. Option (b) is the smallest change but weakens a fail-closed gate, so it needs the argument written down, not assumed. SECOND FIRING, same WI, and the stronger case: after the round-3 verdict was committed the queue refused AGAIN, this time on a merge conflict, because trunk had moved (an unrelated WI filing regenerated the dashboard) and the branch had to merge trunk and resolve two GENERATED artifacts (PROJECT_STATE.html, docs/gate) by regeneration - a merge that altered no WI-280 content whatsoever (all seventeen artifacts blob-identical, verified) yet staled the APPROVE and bought a FOURTH round. The reviewer's framing is the one to design against: under `autonomous` the number of verdict rounds a WI owes is bounded by TRUNK'S COMMIT RATE, not by its own risk. It also observed that option (b)'s exclusion list arguably wants regenerated artifacts in it too, since re-reviewing a generated file is re-reviewing the generator. Practical mitigation already learned and worth writing down whichever option wins: do the close ceremony and any ratification BEFORE the final verdict round, and do not commit trunk-side regenerations while a branch is awaiting merge."

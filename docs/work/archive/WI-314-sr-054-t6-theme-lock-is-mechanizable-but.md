The design call that made it checkable: T6's measurable content is FAMILY PAIRING, not the absence of literals - a fixed status swatch under fixed white ink is theme-locked correctly, since neither side flips. So the test derives the varying/invariant families from the emitted document (the dark block's override set IS the definition) and forbids the two shapes that actually invert a page: a theme mechanism scoped below :root, and a mixed-family surface/ink pair - in CSS rules, on every emitted SVG node, and for CSS-declared text fills paired against the family their host nodes really paint. A sixth check closes the hole the pair checks structurally cannot see: a background rect carries no ink of its own, so every rect fill must be a theme token or a declared vocabulary member, never an ad-hoc locked panel. Swept over every emitter (_every_emitter_document), because a walk judges only what its fixture renders. Cross-document accumulation was needed for the text-fill half: in ONE document 'this selector has no host rect' cannot distinguish 'paints on the page background' from 'this emitter did not render those nodes' - #ice .cell is exactly the latter in the shipped artifact since WI-306. Measured clean on first measurement, the first anchor in this series that was; proven to bite on five independent mutations, each caught by its own assertion (dropped --text in the dark block; a phase block's ink switched to var(--text) over its fixed fill; #ice .cell text switched to var(--text) over its invariant host; a second prefers-color-scheme block scoped to .detail; a node rect repainted #ffffff).

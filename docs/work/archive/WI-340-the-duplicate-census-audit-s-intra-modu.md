All 64 same-file blocks re-triaged by READING them - the dump script printed each block's two extents from the token stream, and the classification came from the source, never from the path pair. They split into 20 semantic classes with THREE dispositions, which is the finding: the old single `intra-module` bucket was not merely coarse, it was wrong about what most of its members are. 34 blocks are ordinary EXTRACTION work that no decomposition programme has to precede (filed as WI-343 agent_dispatch ref plumbing 12, WI-344 check_trajectory staged-close scan 5, WI-345 agent_loop verdict plumbing 4, WI-346 gen_trajectory local spine loader 9, WI-347 four one-offs). 20 really are WI-280's programme - the train disposition arms and the graph/render layer, both of which its row names. 7 are DELIBERATE and were never debt: 2 are the detector matching a parameter list against its own echo (print_run_banner's definition vs its call site; planner_pair's keyword tail vs planner_fallback's deliberately uniform one), 3 are the bundle-unpack preamble, 1 is main()'s per-check warn/strict line whose per-check comment is the point, 1 is a two-armed fold with different accumulators. The census multiset was proven unchanged by the regrouping before anything else ran.

## What the round changed about the recommendation

Before the round the plan said: rebuild in five phases, about 27 days. After
it: **measure first, then decide.** Phase 0 now ends in a two-week control
period on the loop as it stands after the 09-04/05 churn fixes, plus the small
intake improvement already drafted elsewhere; the rebuild proceeds only if the
control period does not deliver the plan's own targets. The spine prune is
preceded by a clause-level preservation mapping and is roughly twice the
effort first stated. The stage clamp and the automatic re-baseline are gone.

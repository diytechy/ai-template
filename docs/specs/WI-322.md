# WI-322 — Give the pending projection its depth (format ruled by OI-10)

**Status: queued**, and **gated on OI-10** — this spec builds whichever option the
owner rules; it does not pick one. The brief, options and recommendation live in
[open-items.md](../open-items.md)'s `OI-10` section.

## The observation

The owner opened a re-attestation sitting, read the generated **Pending owner
actions** block in `open-items.md`, and could not act from it. Two reasons, and
only one of them is a design choice:

1. **Four lines for six rows** — correct by design (the SR is the attestation
   unit, so a `Modified` LLR/TC rides its owning SR's line;
   `gen_trajectory._spine_pending`), but nothing on the line SAYS that, so it
   reads as a projection that lost two rows.
2. **The line is a pointer, not a brief.** It names the command that produces
   the before/after. The owner's expectation is that the decision surface
   carries the context needed to decide — which is precisely what
   `open-items.md`'s own header promises: *"one section per pending decision,
   with the context needed to rule"*.

And the honest constraint underneath: the depth that makes a re-attest readable
is a **word-level diff** — showing that of a 1,500-character `Detail` cell,
forty words moved. Markdown cannot express that. A markdown brief of six amended
rows is the wall of text that prompted this.

## Scope, once ruled

- Whatever the ruled format, the **generator** owns it — the surface stays
  generated-not-hand-maintained and freshness-gated by the harness `status-map`
  step, exactly as the pointer block is today.
- The **attestation-unit fact must be visible**: a projected SR line states how
  many chain rows re-attest with it, and names them.
- The **word-level diff** is the payload: unchanged text collapsible, additions
  and deletions marked, both themes, and a per-cell "how much moved" figure.
- **No second source of truth.** The rendered view derives from the same CSV
  comparison `trace.py --ratify` already performs; if the two ever disagree, the
  brief is authoritative and the renderer is the defect.

## Hazard to carry into the build, whichever option wins

The baseline is the whole ballgame. `--ratify modified` with no `--since` returns
an EMPTY section for a row whose amendment landed while it still read `Verified`
(SR-052 and SR-053 today), and a badly chosen `--since` fabricates one. A
rendered view inherits both failure modes and makes them prettier, so it must
show the baseline revision it diffed against, and say plainly when a section is
empty rather than rendering a confident blank.

## Done-when

- [ ] The ruled surface renders all `Modified` rows — including the chain rows
      that ride an SR's line — with per-cell before/after.
- [ ] The baseline revision is stated on every section, and an empty section
      reads as *check the baseline*, never as *nothing changed*.
- [ ] It regenerates from one command and is freshness-gated like its siblings.
- [ ] `docs/status.md` and `open-items.md` point at it in one place each.

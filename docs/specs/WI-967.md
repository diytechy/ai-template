## Problem

WI-963 (the trunk mint drops amendment records a merged range already
settled) passed its checkpoint, and its gate raised one convergence-class
MAJOR that dispute sitting 007-ADJUDICATE-1ae50f4 ruled FIX: a traced change
routed after a row's act settles silently when a later act in the range
re-copies that registry for another row. The sound remedy needs to know
which registries each act copied, and the act ledger
(`docs/archive/last_approved/acts.toml`, IF-220) does not say. The snapshot
writer appends one entry per act with only `seq`, `date`, `approved` and
`reattested`. Three act shapes copy a registry the entry never names (wi-963
D-011, probed through the real producer):

- a traced re-anchor (`intake.py snapshot --approves <registry>=<ref>` with
  no ids) copies its registry and writes `approved = []`, `reattested = []`;
- an act that mixes `--approves` on one registry with ids in another lists
  only the second;
- a re-seed copies every registry and lists only newly approved rows.

Comparing against the copy the re-attesting act's own commit wrote (the fix1
build) does not close it either: every hand landing is a `git merge
--squash`, whose one commit is the act's commit.

## Scope

Each act-ledger entry records the registries its act copied, for every act
shape the snapshot writer produces: the entry's shape (IF-220), its one
writer, and its validators, with one RESYNC entry for adopters' ledgers.
WI-963's settlement rule is **not** in scope; WI-963 rebases onto this
row's landing and builds it there.

Questions the scope critique answers before the claim:

- how an entry written before this row is read (the ledger is
  append-only and says "Do not edit");
- every reader of the ledger, found by grep (`acceptance_record`,
  `baseline_snapshot`, `gen_open_items` today), and whether the file's
  authority over acts needs a `## Trust` section (rubric S2);
- the landing order: it changes the act ledger the cycle runs on, so it
  lands alone (revision 37).

# WI-232 — Make needs-re-review actionable

**Branch:** dualplan-routing-fix · **Filed:** 2026-07-18 (parallel-run field
finding 3) · **BuildTier:** medium · **SafetyClass:** high-risk (dispatcher
control flow / stop semantics).

## Why

Composition's contract says a textual conflict "demands a focused re-review"
([agent_dispatch.py:930-942](../../project-trajectory/scripts/agent_dispatch.py#L930-L942)),
but nothing ever dispatches one: `needs-re-review` is only an attention state
([agent_dispatch.py:1958-1970](../../project-trajectory/scripts/agent_dispatch.py#L1958-L1970)).
Each relaunch reconciles the train `ready-to-integrate`, mechanically retries
the identical 3-way merge, re-parks it, and stops again — while burning every
other resumable lane (field evidence, run `20260718T190911`: WI-228 and
WI-219 re-parked byte-identically within seconds of launch, plus a ~7-minute
WI-229 quarantine cycle). The stop banner never says human resolution is
required, so an operator reasonably relaunches expecting progress. The
2026-07-18 wrap-up was ultimately a *manual* merge outside the dispatcher.

## Scope

Decide-then-implement, one of:

- **(a) A real re-review lane:** dispatch a focused worker session into the
  train's integrate worktree carrying the conflict context (conflicted paths,
  both sides, the integrated head), with a bounded retry budget before
  escalating to NEEDS-HUMAN.
- **(b) Rule it human work and say so:** a parked conflicted train surfaces
  run-state NEEDS-HUMAN with an `ask:` line naming the train and conflicted
  paths (the WI-127 contract), and reconcile skips re-attempting a train
  whose merge inputs (train tip, integration head) are unchanged since the
  recorded conflict — the retry loop becomes idempotent instead of silent.

Spec recommendation: **(b) first** — small, honest, and WI-231 should already
eliminate the generated-file majority of parks; revisit (a) only if parks
stay frequent afterwards. The ruling lands in the spec + the canonical
PROCESS_OPTIONS unattended section, WI-223-style.

**Non-goals:** no auto-resolution of conflicts (WI-231 owns the generated
set; source conflicts stay human/re-review work); no new journal schema
beyond recording the conflict inputs for the idempotence guard.

## Done-when

A parked conflicted train either receives its focused session (a) or pages
NEEDS-HUMAN with the named ask (b); a relaunch with unchanged merge inputs
does not re-run the failing merge; a relaunch after the inputs change (new
integration head or amended train) retries once. Regression drives a real
conflict through park → relaunch → page/dispatch. Suite stays green.

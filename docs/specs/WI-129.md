# WI-129 — LLR/TC status-coherence warn (registry lint)

**Status: queued (owner-filed 2026-07-13, at the WI-128 sitting).** The owner
asked for the LLR-status semantics to be either fixed or warned on, after the
v2 slices left LLR-051/052 at `Implemented` while their TCs and SRs read
`Verified` — mechanically harmless (LLR status never independently gates under
the derived-gate model) but confusing at a ratification review, and enforced
today by prose only.

## Facts (as of filing)

- `derive_gate.maturity_gate` deliberately ignores LLR/TC Status past `Draft`;
  the SR's `Verified` drives G2→G3. So an LLR stuck below `Verified` can never
  block a gate, a check, or a run — the drift is purely a readout problem.
- All 50 v1 LLRs were lifted to `Verified` by their dev slices; the two v2
  slices didn't lift theirs (corrected by WI-128). Nothing mechanical caught
  the difference.
- `trace.py` already joins the LLR and TC registries and owns the
  warn/finding vocabulary (orphans, integrity, `--strict-integrity`), and
  shares the case-insensitive `is_verified()` predicate pinned equal across
  scripts by `tests/test_rule_sync.py` (WI-101/M1).

## Design (recommended)

**Warn, don't mutate — and don't gate.**

- In `trace.py`, after the join: for each LLR whose Status is not Verified
  (via the shared `is_verified()`) but which is cited by at least one TC and
  **all** of whose citing TCs are Verified, emit one warn-tier finding:
  `LLR-xxx reads '<status>' but every citing TC is Verified — lift to
  Verified (the evidence already exists)`. Included in the report
  (`docs/test/report.md`) and stdout.
- **Never** promoted to an error — not under `--strict`, not under
  `--strict-integrity`. Rationale: the lint mirrors the derived-gate stance
  that LLR status is non-gating; making it blocking would re-introduce
  through the back door exactly the coupling the gate model removed. (If
  field use shows the warn gets ignored, a promotion flag can be a follow-up
  ruling.)
- The **auto-lift / derived-status alternative is rejected**: the registries
  are hand-owned SSOT surfaces; generators derive *from* them (`docs/gate`,
  the dashboard) and never write registry cells back. A tool silently editing
  Status would blur who asserts verification.
- One line in the `registry-hygiene` skill's findings list so the fixer knows
  the warn and the fix (edit the Status cell). No PROCESS.md change (byte
  budget; the rule is self-explanatory in the warn text).

## Done-when

1. A fixture with an `Implemented` LLR whose only citing TC is `Verified`
   produces exactly the warn; lifting the LLR silences it.
2. Quiet cases covered: LLR already Verified; LLR whose citing TCs are not
   all Verified; LLR with no citing TC (that's the existing orphan finding's
   job, not this lint's).
3. Status matching is case-insensitive via the shared predicate (stays pinned
   by `test_rule_sync`).
4. `--strict` / `--strict-integrity` exit codes unchanged by the new warn
   (asserted in the tests).
5. Tests live in `tests/test_trace.py`; commit bar green.

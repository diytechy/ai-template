# WI-123 — Review cadence dial: campaign-close adversarial rounds (owner proposal)

**Status: queued (owner-proposed 2026-07-12 evening, ruling pending —
recommendation: evidence-gate, don't adopt yet).** The owner asked: instead
of a review after each committed session, should the basis be **two
adversarial reviews at the end of a campaign**, with the passing test suite
as the general protection in between?

## Facts (as of filing)

- Today the review round fires after **every committed non-review session**;
  `docs/review-policy` dials how many reviewers per round (0/1/2, split
  charters at 2) — it is a **breadth** dial, not a **cadence** dial. There is
  no campaign-close review; campaign close today is the gate bar
  (`check.py --gate <gate>`) only.
- The S8 machinery (escalation ladder, scoreboard, tripwires, contradiction
  detection) keys on per-round verdicts; under `single-ratify` the per-slice
  REVIEW-A verdicts are the LLM-gate legs the owner's batch ratification
  leans on.
- Observed cost (2026-07-12 run): a review is the **cheap** leg — ~$2.26 /
  ~9 min (opus) vs ~$13–18 / 29–38 min per strong BUILD.

## Why "tests pass" is not sufficient cover here

The builder **writes its own tests**. A green suite cannot police the two
failure modes the per-slice reviews actually catch in this repo's record: a
**vacuous TC** (review 006-A explicitly verified "the Verified TC is honest,
not vacuous") and an **overstated claim** (the 003-A round caught registry
hygiene the harness could not). Tests protect against regression in what is
tested; reviews protect the honesty of what "tested" means. Campaign-close
review also finds defects N slices deep, where rework is N× the price.

## The coupling that matters

WI-121 just relaxed BUILD to the medium tier, and that relax is safe
**because** every slice is reviewed — tier-up-never-down re-raises a
contested build to strong only when a review round contests it. Removing the
per-slice round in the same breath removes the escalation sensor the relax
leans on. Do not turn both dials at once.

## Recommended shape (if adopted after evidence)

Evidence gate (the WI-110 pattern): run ≥ 2 campaigns with medium BUILDs and
per-slice reviews ON; adopt only if the verdicts run ≥ ~90% APPROVE with no
escalations. Then:

- A **cadence** value beside the breadth dial (e.g. `docs/review-cadence`:
  `slice` (default, today's behavior) | `campaign`), never-breaking (absent =
  `slice`).
- `campaign` = **2 reviewers, split charters, cross-family** (WI-120 makes
  the OPENAI leg reachable) at the campaign/`[g*]` close, plus
  **tripwire-triggered early rounds** mid-campaign (big diff, template/spine
  files touched) so the escalation sensor never fully sleeps.
- Define close-time CHANGES-REQUESTED semantics (which WIs reopen; how the
  escalation ladder maps to a multi-slice range) before wiring.

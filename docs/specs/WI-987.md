# WI-987 — The open-items page lists the needs owing an act (spec of record)

**WI:** WI-987 · **Status:** queued · **Delivers:** SR-178 (anchor)

The row keeps its Context and Done-when, the one home of what closes it;
this spec holds the problem. Its scope critique settles the rest before
the claim.

## Problem

SR-178: "The kit shall report any recorded artifact whose text has moved
away from the copy recording its acceptance - stakeholder needs included -
regardless of any Status movement."

The detector meets it. `baseline_snapshot.needs_owing` reports SN-028 and
SN-036 as `DRIFTED` on trunk at `43bfdfc35`, and the Markdown approval brief
renders both (`trace.need_brief_lines`, "Stakeholder needs and stakeholders
owing an act").

The owner's page does not. Section 2 of `docs/open-items.html` ("Approval &
re-attestation") renders only `trace.reattest_model`, which is keyed by SR
and walks each SR's LLRs and TCs; nothing above an SR reaches it.
`gen_open_items` never calls `needs_owing`, so the section prints its empty
message, "no SR owes an approval or a re-attest". It is literally true and
reads as nothing owed. The two needs show on the page only as the prose of
the OI-117 and OI-118 cards.

The generator's own docstring rules on the disagreement: "if this view and
the `--approve` brief ever disagree, the brief is authoritative and this is
the bug."

## Scope

The page reads `needs_owing` and renders its rows in section 2, the way the
brief does. The detector does not change.

Questions the scope critique answers before the claim:

- whether the page also renders the other tiers `baseline_snapshot.tier_owing`
  serves the amendment brief (assumptions and surrogates), or only the needs
  file's two tiers SR-178 names;
- whether the dashboard (`PROJECT_STATE.html`) or `pending.py` repeats the
  same SR-only reading, found by grep, and whether that is this row's or a
  successor's.

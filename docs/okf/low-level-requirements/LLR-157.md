# LLR-157 — The second axis: which tier is in process

**Detail.** `spine_stage` returns the LOWEST unfinished tier, where a MISSING artifact belongs to its own tier rather than to its parent (an SR with no LLR yet is stage 2, because what is being written is an LLR), `Modified` is the SR's own tier and is checked FIRST, and any other not-yet-Verified SR is checked LAST because a child in flight is the honest answer while it is in flight. `stage_to_gate` states the mapping to the harness gate once, deliberately lossy in one direction only. The value rides `docs/gate` as an APPENDED basis field.

**Rationale.** Reading a missing child as the parent's tier made stages 2 and 3 unreachable during exactly the period they describe, leaving the axis unable to express the distinction it exists for. Appending rather than inserting the basis field is required: `check.py`'s basis regex depends on the existing field order.

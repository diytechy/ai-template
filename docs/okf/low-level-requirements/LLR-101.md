# LLR-101 — Non-pruning SVG container roles (A2 core)

**Detail.** The MECHANIZED core of accessibility anchor A2 (docs/rubrics/dashboard-accessibility.md), split out of LLR-053 under the WI-300 (f) ruling and delivered by WI-297: no emitted <svg> declares the CHILDREN-PRESENTATIONAL role=img over focusable descendants. ARIA expects a role=img subtree pruned from the accessibility tree, so declaring it over an interactive graph hides the per-node <title>s A2 rests on and may make the nodes unreachable. The role is chosen from the emitted BODY by _svg_role (group when the body holds any tabindex, else img), so a future emitter adding focusable nodes cannot silently reintroduce the conflict; a remaining role=img must carry its own accessible name. SCOPE: the container-role invariant over the whole emitted document. Whether each individual control READS as well-named remains perceptual under LLR-053/TC-053.

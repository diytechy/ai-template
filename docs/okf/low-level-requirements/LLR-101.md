# LLR-101 — Non-pruning SVG container roles (A2 core)

**Detail.** The mechanized core of accessibility anchor A2 (docs/rubrics/dashboard-accessibility.md): no emitted <svg> declares the CHILDREN-PRESENTATIONAL role=img over a FOCUSABLE descendant, and any svg that does declare role=img carries its own accessible name (aria-label, or a first-direct-child <title> — per SVG-AAM a <title> nested in a <g> names the <g>, not the svg). Focusable means tabindex in any quoting OR a native SVG <a href>, which is tab-ordered without a tabindex. The role is chosen from the emitted BODY by _svg_role. SCOPE — and no wider: the container-role invariant as PROVEN by TC-104, over the artifact this repo ships plus the tiered fixture plus the predicate's own unit cases; it is NOT a proof over every reachable emitter configuration, so an emitter no rendered artifact exercises is unguarded. Name QUALITY belongs to LLR-108/TC-113.

**Rationale.** role=img prunes its subtree from the accessibility tree, so declaring it over focusable descendants hides every one of them from assistive technology while the page still looks correct. The predicate must treat a native SVG <a href> as focusable and not tabindex alone: a tabindex-only predicate passes a diagram of linked stage cards that are tab-ordered without one. Setting the role at each call site was rejected — deriving it from the body is what stops a future emitter silently reintroducing the conflict.

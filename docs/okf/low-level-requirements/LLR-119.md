# LLR-119 — T4 label legibility, mechanized: ink inside its box, truncation with a reveal

**Detail.** The mechanized floor of usability anchor T4 (docs/rubrics/dashboard-usability.md), widening the anchor's existing owner rather than adding a rubric anchor. The clause made objective: no character a block renders may fall outside that block's own rect, horizontally or vertically. The emitter FITS the sub-label to the column it will actually be drawn in — a second line when one will not hold it, then an ellipsis — with three text lines the ceiling, reusing the grid the id/name wrap idiom already proved fits row_h; a sub-label that already fits one line renders byte-identically to before. SCOPE — PROVEN: for every block of every drill layer in the emitted document, each label line's ink box lies inside its own node rect, measured from the emitted geometry with the emitter's declared per-character widths and the document's declared font sizes, entities counted as the single glyph they render. Second half: this emitter does not truncate the next-work title by a fixed budget — the card emits the whole clause and lets its own width do the fitting, the declared bound _NEXT_WORK_TITLE caps ONE item's HEIGHT rather than its text, and where it bites the remainder discloses through a NATIVE <details> cut at a word so head and remainder rejoin continuously when opened. DELIBERATELY NOT: whether an ellipsis a reader meets is ACTIONABLE — truncated-without-affordance stays T4's perceptual residue under LLR-055/TC-055, so T4 remains a live critique anchor; the full string keeps its homes in the block's title, aria-label and data-summary.

**Rationale.** A label is illegible AS a label the moment its ink leaves the box it names, which is the half of this anchor a reader cannot argue with. Column right-sizing (LLR-057) cannot absorb it: right-sizing clamps at MAX_TIER_COL, so a 400-character need asks for a 2000px column and gets 172 — the overflow is structural, not a tuning problem. A FIXED character budget was the shipped approach on the next-work card and is rejected: it spends the same budget at every width, cutting a title mid-word on a wide screen with the card half empty. Native <details> is the point of the reveal — operable by pointer AND keyboard with no script, in a file opened from disk, which is what makes the affordance assertable from the markup instead of needing a browser. The height bound is measured over 320 registry rows: median 44, p90 126, max 609 characters of leading clause.

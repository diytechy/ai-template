# LLR-119 — No node-label ink outside its own block (T4 geometric floor)

**Detail.** The MECHANIZED floor of usability anchor T4 (docs/rubrics/dashboard-usability.md), bound by WI-318 under the WI-300 (f) pattern and WIDENING the anchor's existing owner rather than adding a rubric anchor. The clause made objective is the one a reader cannot argue with: a label is illegible AS a label the moment its ink leaves the box it names, so no character a block renders may fall outside that block's own rect, horizontally or vertically. 121-CRITIQUE's MAJOR was the whole of a stakeholder need drawn as one unwrapped sub-label line, starting outside the left edge of its node and ending past the right one at every captured width. Column right-sizing (LLR-057, SR-056) cannot absorb that: it clamps at MAX_TIER_COL, so a 400-character need asks for a 2000px column and gets 172. The emitter therefore FITS the sub-label to the column it will actually be drawn in - a second line when one will not hold it, then an ellipsis - with three text lines the ceiling, the grid the id/name wrap idiom already proved fits row_h; a sub-label that already fits one line renders byte-identically to before. SCOPE - PROVEN: for every block of every drill layer in the emitted document, each label line's ink box lies inside its own node rect, measured from the emitted geometry with the emitter's declared per-character widths and the document's declared font sizes, entities counted as the single glyph they render. DELIBERATELY NOT: whether an ellipsis a reader meets is ACTIONABLE - truncated-WITHOUT-AFFORDANCE stays T4's perceptual residue under LLR-055/TC-055 (the full string keeps its homes in the block's title, aria-label and data-summary), so T4 remains a live critique anchor.

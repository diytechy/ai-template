# LLR-107 — One interaction idiom per node role (U4 core)

**Detail.** The mechanized core of uniformity anchor U4 (docs/rubrics/dashboard-uniformity.md). Two node roles exist and each is spelled ONE way in every emitter that renders it: an IDENTIFIED node (icicle .cell, flat-DAG .wi, knowledge .knode) advertises its registry id through `data-id`; a DETAIL-BEARING drill node (.block, in both the When and How-SW drills) advertises its detail-map key through `data-node`; and a node of either role is made focusable the same way, `tabindex="0"` on the group. Neither role ever carries the other's attribute. SCOPE: ATTRIBUTE parity, not runtime behaviour parity.

**Rationale.** One node addressed two ways means every later consumer must handle both, and the second spelling is the one that gets forgotten. Proving that two emitters' handlers BEHAVE alike was considered and rejected as out of reach without a browser harness — what is provable from the markup is that they are wired through one vocabulary, and that is the narrowing this row accepts explicitly rather than claiming more than it checks.

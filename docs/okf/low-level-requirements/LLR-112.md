# LLR-112 — Keyboard reachability of every wired control (A1 core)

**Detail.** The MECHANIZED core of accessibility anchor A1 (docs/rubrics/dashboard-accessibility.md), split out of LLR-053 under the WI-300 (f) ruling and delivered by WI-313: every element the emitted page WIRES an interaction to is keyboard-focusable. The wired set is DERIVED from the document's own querySelectorAll calls and then classified - a control selector (its matches receive per-element click/focus/dblclick/keydown listeners) may match only elements carrying tabindex=0 or natively tab-ordered (a button; an SVG a-with-href, the LLR-101 lesson), and each non-control selector records why its matches are not focus stops. A newly wired selector fails the owning test until classified, which is the closure the earlier per-element A1 tests (drill leaves, the WI-273 tablist) never gave. The rubric's 'sensible order' clause is narrowed to what is assertable, per the ruling's recorded recommendation: document order IS emission order, no positive tabindex may reorder the walk, and tabindex=-1 is legal only on the WI-273 roving tablist buttons. SCOPE - and no wider: focusability and order-integrity of the wired set over the shipped artifact plus the seven-fixture emitter sweep. Enter/Space ACTIVATION is wired by the same emitted controllers but its runtime behaviour is not driven here (no browser harness), and whether the order FEELS sensible stays a reader-experience judgement, deliberately dropped.

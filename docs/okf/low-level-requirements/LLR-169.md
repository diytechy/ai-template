# LLR-169 — Retired-vocabulary enforcer

**Detail.** Walks the tree, classifies each file through in_scope (declared carve-out globs for historical, generated and attestation-quoting surfaces, plus a text-suffix filter), and reports one finding per offending LINE - not per tag, since a line naming three tags is one sentence to rewrite. Matching is TAG-SCOPED (a whole-word alternation over the retired G tags), never the word 'gate', which survives wherever it means a check that can fail. Declaration sites opt out through an explicit in-file marker rather than a heuristic, because a heuristic that can be satisfied by accident is how the vocabulary grows back through the enforcer meant to stop it. Wired into check.py at EVERY bar with --strict added from DevBar-Tests on, mirroring check_trajectory's severity ladder: the surfaces the tags regrow into are authored hardest at the lowest bar.

# LLR-111 — Declared type scale, no raw font-size literals (U1 residue)

**Detail.** Mechanizes what LLR-104 left perceptual. 'Do the sizes read as visually uniform' was undecidable because the scale was never written down: 18 raw font-size literals against 5 tokens, with .7/.75rem, .9/.95/.98rem, 1.05/1.1rem and 8.5/9px each being near-duplicate steps for ONE role 3-7% apart. Eleven steps are now declared in THREE documented families - node (px, because SVG geometry is fixed px and a rem resizes labels out of their boxes), page (rem), and one relative step (em, for text sized against its parent) - and every emitted font-size must resolve to one. Claiming a single scale across those units would be false, so the LLR states three. SCOPE: step membership over paint surfaces. Whether the chosen steps are well-proportioned is not asserted.

# LLR-158 — The ledger's three rungs and their tiers

**Detail.** `normative_text` reuses `spine_cell_class` rather than a second column list, so the digest and the amend-without-flip warn cannot disagree about what "normative" means; `attestation_findings` is warn/strict like the other registry findings and also reports a ledger row anchoring nothing; `attestation_integrity_findings` and the append-only guard are ALWAYS hard. The guard reads its two trees with `--no-renames` so a moved ledger shows its old path, and reports REMOVAL explicitly.

**Rationale.** Integrity is hard because a ledger that does not parse is not an anchor at all and every reader silently falls back to the git-history derivation it replaces. Removal was the gap: it silenced all three rungs at once, so the strongest form of rewriting was the one form the guard could not see.

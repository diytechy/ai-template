# LLR-135 — Paraphrase advisory

**Detail.** paraphrase_advisories compares a child cell's significant words (alphabetic runs of four characters or more, lowercased) against its parent's and reports when the share of the CHILD's words that also appear in the parent exceeds the declared threshold — the child is the denominator, so a long parent cannot dilute a short echoing child. Reported in its own advisory pipe, which never joins a failure set. The two watched relationships are an SR Rationale against its own Requirement and an LLR Detail against its parent SR Requirement — the pairs where the parent is unambiguous; each finding emits a stdout WARNING plus a report line with the exit code unchanged.

**Rationale.** A child that re-words its parent adds a second home for one fact and no decomposition, so a later amendment updates one copy and the two disagree with nothing to report it. The advisory has its own pipe rather than sharing the acceptance-criteria counter because a shared count would report 'ac-advisories' about a finding that is not one, and a reader acts on the counter name.

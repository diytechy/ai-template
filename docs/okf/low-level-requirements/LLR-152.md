# LLR-152 — The adjudication kind: rank-1 slot + no-bar arm + the bar strictness key

**Detail.** The `adjudication` kind (docs/concurrency-v2.md §A5.2) joins the declared SafetyClass vocabulary: exclusive on the concurrency axis, rank 1 on the order axis - the slot §A1 reserved, filled without renumbering the ruled table. The kind's lane runs NO product bar (integrate._lane_bar_directives + _refresh_bar: the refresh still merges trunk in, runs the trunk step and commits a verified Bar-Green attestation whose summary reads no-bar honestly) because its outputs are Status cells and the work registry, nothing a product bar can speak to; a mixed batch or unreadable frontmatter fails TOWARD the bar. The optional `bar = G1|G2|G3` frontmatter key rides the same directive read: bar declares verification strictness for this row's lane; it never affects scheduling (load_wis deliberately does not parse it) - the refresh passes the batch's strictest value to check.py --gate and refuses a malformed one loudly.

**Rationale.** Narrowing the amendment detector to ratified cells without the kind would move mis-fires from spurious window to MISSED window; the two are a pair (docs/concurrency-v2.md §A5.2).

# LLR-109 — Pairwise deltaE floor across every vocabulary (U5 residue)

**Detail.** Mechanizes what LLR-102 leaves perceptual: a PAIRWISE deltaE floor across every declared vocabulary, not only within PHASE_ACCENTS. Two floors, each a recorded judgement — 15 WITHIN a vocabulary (every member can sit beside every other in one legend) and 12 ACROSS (two vocabularies share no legend, so the floor is lower rather than absent). The declared tier<->okf mirror is exempt BY DESIGN and is asserted BYTE-EQUAL, so 'exempt' cannot drift into 'nearly equal'. SCOPE: perceptual SEPARATION of the declared palettes. Whether a given hue suits its concept is not asserted.

**Rationale.** 'A collision the identity check misses' looked like a judgement call only because the formula was already there and nobody had widened the set it ran over — the same arithmetic on more members. A cross-vocabulary floor of zero was rejected on evidence: a reader conflated a pair drawn from two different vocabularies, so sharing no legend reduces the risk without removing it. Three live conflations were sitting in the shipped palettes at the binding — sw external/component at deltaE 8.6, SR/phase[3] at 9.5, Interface/phase[7] at 10.5.

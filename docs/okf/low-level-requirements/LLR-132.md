# LLR-132 — PAGE ratification-level mapping

**Detail.** Applies the declared level's continuation semantics to a PAGE at both the dispatcher disposition and the --dual-plan single-shot round: `page_action(human_held, keep_nondependent)` over the four cells the retired enum's three words could not span — a human-held tier pages, a loop-held one STALLs, and `None` normalizes to the strictest cell rather than to `bool(None)`'s most permissive one.

**Rationale.** Split from the disposition row: committing the outcome and deciding whether it stops a human are independent, and the second is shared with a second entry point that would otherwise drift.

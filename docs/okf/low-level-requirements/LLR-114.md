# LLR-114 — Text-contrast floors, closed over the vocabulary set (A4)

**Detail.** Accessibility anchor A4 bound, split out of LLR-053 under the WI-300 (f) ruling (WI-313). The WCAG 2.1 AA arithmetic was already owned by six tests - node fills vs label ink at 4.5:1, the emitted badge/boundary pairs, the no-opacity-discount rule, theme-token fills behind white text in BOTH themes (WI-293), the hub-is-not-accent regression, and the WI-299 per-fill focus ring at the 3:1 boundary floor - so the work here is the BINDING plus the one gap no test owned: the SET. Each sibling sweeps a hand-copied list of the palette constants, so a sixth vocabulary could ship outside every floor check. Closed by reflection: every UPPERCASE module-level collection holding hex members must be one of the five declared vocabularies, and every member of the REFLECTED set clears 4.5:1 against its label ink (white, except the one declared queued dark-ink exception) and 3:1 for its computed focus ring - a new vocabulary enters the arithmetic by existing, not by being remembered. SCOPE - and no wider: contrast arithmetic over declared constants and emitted theme tokens (the ring boundary also binds under SR-054 T5 as LLR-105). Rendered-pixel measurement - antialiasing, overlap, real zoom - is not asserted; that would need a browser harness.

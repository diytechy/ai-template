# LLR-114 — Text-contrast floors, closed over the vocabulary set (A4)

**Detail.** Accessibility anchor A4 bound. The WCAG 2.1 AA arithmetic is owned by six sibling tests — node fills vs label ink at 4.5:1, the emitted badge/boundary pairs, the no-opacity-discount rule, theme-token fills behind white text in BOTH themes, the hub-is-not-accent regression, and the per-fill focus ring at the 3:1 boundary floor — so this row adds the BINDING plus the gap none of them owned: the SET. Closed by reflection: every UPPERCASE module-level collection holding hex members must be one of the five declared vocabularies, and every member of the REFLECTED set clears 4.5:1 against its label ink (white, except the one declared queued dark-ink exception) and 3:1 for its computed focus ring. The walk is RECURSIVE (nested dicts, tuples, sets, bare strings), counts any 3/4/6/8-digit hex form as hex-bearing, and requires every member be CANONICAL 6-digit lowercase. The theme-token half is DERIVED from the emitted CSS: every fill:var(--x) token must carry a declared role, white-text-fill roles clear 4.5:1 under white in BOTH themes, container-fill roles keep --text readable in both. SCOPE — and no wider: contrast arithmetic over declared constants and emitted theme tokens (the ring boundary also binds under SR-054 T5 as LLR-105); a lowercase module-level palette would evade the reflection and is forbidden by the module's UPPERCASE-constant convention, a recorded reliance. Rendered-pixel measurement is not asserted.

**Rationale.** Each sibling test sweeps a hand-copied list of the palettes, so a sixth vocabulary could ship outside every floor check while all six stayed green — the arithmetic was right and the SET was the hole. A new vocabulary must enter the arithmetic by EXISTING rather than by being remembered, which is what reflection buys and a seventh hand-copied list would not. Two shapes of that same defect must not return: a flat-dict-only walk misses nested and non-dict collections, and a hand-listed theme-token set is the very defect this row claims to close — both were live here, which is why the walk is recursive and the token set derived.

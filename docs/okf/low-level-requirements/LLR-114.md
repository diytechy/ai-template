# LLR-114 — Text-contrast floors, closed over the vocabulary set (A4)

**Detail.** Accessibility anchor A4 bound, split out of LLR-053 under the (f) decomposition ruling. The WCAG 2.1 AA arithmetic was already owned by six tests - node fills vs label ink at 4.5:1, the emitted badge/boundary pairs, the no-opacity-discount rule, theme-token fills behind white text in BOTH themes, the hub-is-not-accent regression, and the per-fill focus ring at the 3:1 boundary floor - so the work here is the BINDING plus the one gap no test owned: the SET. Each sibling sweeps a hand-copied list of the palette constants, so a sixth vocabulary could ship outside every floor check. Closed by reflection: every UPPERCASE module-level collection holding hex members must be one of the five declared vocabularies, and every member of the REFLECTED set clears 4.5:1 against its label ink (white, except the one declared queued dark-ink exception) and 3:1 for its computed focus ring - a new vocabulary enters the arithmetic by existing, not by being remembered. REWORKED same day after adversarial review defeated the flat-dict-only reflection with seven shapes: the walk is now RECURSIVE (nested dicts, tuples, sets, bare strings), counts ANY 3/4/6/8-digit hex form as hex-bearing, and requires every member be CANONICAL 6-digit lowercase so a shorthand or alpha member fails rather than shipping unmeasured. The theme-token half was itself a hand-copied set of one ({--hub}) - the exact defect this LLR claims to close - and is now DERIVED from the emitted CSS: every fill:var(--x) token must carry a declared role, white-text-fill roles clear 4.5:1 under white in BOTH themes, container-fill roles keep --text readable on them in both themes. SCOPE - and no wider: contrast arithmetic over declared constants and emitted theme tokens (the ring boundary also binds under SR-054 T5 as LLR-105); a lowercase module-level palette would evade the reflection and is forbidden by the module's UPPERCASE-constant convention, a recorded reliance. Rendered-pixel measurement - antialiasing, overlap, real zoom - is not asserted; that would need a browser harness.

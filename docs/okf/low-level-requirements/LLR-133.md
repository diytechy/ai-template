# LLR-133 — Spine stand-alone rule

**Detail.** provenance_findings flags a WI id or a process-doc citation across the normative cells of all three spine registries, joining the --strict failure set via exit_code.

**Rationale.** A citation is a pointer a later reader may be unable to follow, and the row's reason travels with it: when the work item is closed and archived, the row keeps its claim and loses its justification, which is the failure this catches. Applied to every spine registry rather than the SR alone, because the layer the rule could not originally see held the largest pocket of violations — a narrower scope would guard the cleanest layer and miss the dirtiest.

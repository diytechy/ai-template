# SR-048 — How-SW top view bounded and containerized

**Requirement.** check_trajectory.py shall bound the software-architecture top view at 10 items - top-level CMP-### components (no PartOf) that contain an arch-map module plus uncontained modules - reporting a finding warn-first and, under --strict (G2+), an error that drives right-sizing of the component designations; and gen_trajectory.py shall render that top view containerized in PROJECT_STATE.html, each component expandable to its member modules, nested components and internal seams with cross-component IF-### seams aggregated to the container boundary. Membership derives from the Component tag on LLR rows (LLR.Module -> CMP-###), nesting via PartOf (a module counts only at its top-level root); docs/components-check: off opts out and a <=10-module or arch-map-less inventory is vacuous.

**Acceptance criteria.** An arch-map inventory over 10 modules with no containing CMP rows warns plain and fails --strict naming the count; declaring a right-sized handful of top-level CMPs that contain the modules clears it; a nested CMP (PartOf) counts only at its top-level root; docs/components-check: off silences it; a <=10-module or arch-map-less inventory is vacuous; the How-SW panel renders the containerized <details> top view with expandable members and one deduplicated component-to-component edge per crossing pair when CMP rows contain modules, and stays the flat view (byte-identical) otherwise; generation is byte-deterministic through --check.

**Rationale.** Realizes SN-023 (the architecture view stays legible - an unbounded module map on the dashboard hides drift as much as scattering the relationships does) and SN-012 (the bound, not the registry, is the rule, so a small or non-adopting repo pays nothing while a 20-module repo is nudged to declare its composition). The AXES-ratified Component-tag membership join already existed; WI-073 makes the top view honor it and the bound enforce right-sizing.

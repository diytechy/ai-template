# SR-123 — Critique closure ratchet

**Requirement.** check_trajectory.py shall warn when a Critique WI closes after CHANGES-REQUESTED without validation evidence changing.

**Acceptance criteria.** The lax-closure case warns, while an approval or a TC, test or rubric delta stays silent.

**Rationale.** Realizes SN-006 — a changes-requested verdict followed by a close with nothing altered is how a critique becomes a formality, and nothing else in the harness can see that pattern. Hard-failing the close was rejected: the evidence sometimes lands outside the registries, so the check reports rather than adjudicates.

# SR-119 — Release on early train end

**Requirement.** On any early end the dispatcher shall transactionally release unstarted constituent reservations and recompute the traincar DAG.

**Acceptance criteria.** An early end (block, conflict or cap) releases unstarted reservations and recomputes the DAG.

**Rationale.** Realizes SN-025 — a train that stops at a blocker holds reservations on constituents it never started, and those WIs are invisible to the frontier until someone clears the refs by hand. Expiring reservations on a timer was rejected: it releases work that is still running as readily as work that stopped.

# SR-003 — Placeholder and schema checks

**Requirement.** trace.py shall, under --no-placeholders, flag any leftover -000 example row, and under --strict-schema, require non-empty required fields and in-vocabulary Verification/Tier values.

**Acceptance criteria.** --no-placeholders reports every -000 row across SR/LLR/TC and the SN doc; --strict-schema reports an empty required field or an out-of-vocabulary Verification/Tier value.

**Rationale.** Realizes the dissolved edge expectation that a committed placeholder at a gate is a finding and SN-002 (a real row missing a required cell breaks the trace).

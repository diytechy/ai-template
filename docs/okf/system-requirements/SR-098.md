# SR-098 — Durable publication intent

**Requirement.** The integrator shall publish through a durable compare-and-swap intent and sync only an exactly clean or expected development worktree while retaining recoverable evidence on failure.

**Acceptance criteria.** Publication is idempotent across either CAS crash boundary; disjoint dirt is preserved, overlapping dirt defers, non-descendant targets fail, and the intent proves recovery state.

**Rationale.** Realizes SN-025;SN-008 by isolating one independently reviewable obligation formerly carried by SR-063.

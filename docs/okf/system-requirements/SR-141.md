# SR-141 — The loop's priority order is stated and pinned

**Requirement.** Admission shall prefer `adjudication` rows over all other ready work, as a stable partition applied at admission rather than by renumbering the ruled rank table, and shall not thereby admit anything past a human-held stop.

**Acceptance criteria.** An adjudication row admits ahead of a ready spine batch; the ruled rank table is unchanged, so `--explain` still reports it; the partition is stable (non-adjudication rows keep their relative order); a human-held attestation still drains and surfaces with the adjudication row waiting behind the stop.

**Rationale.** A ratification is the one act this loop cannot cheaply take back — the ledger is append-only by design — so a spine batch that re-attests requirements must not run ahead of an unjudged claim that may overturn its premise. Renumbering the ruled rank table would migrate every downstream repo's ordering for a rung that belongs to admission, so the two orders are kept distinct and the disagreement is stated.

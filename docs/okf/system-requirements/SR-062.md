# SR-062 — Change-train continuation

**Requirement.** Within an assigned traincar the dispatcher shall continue the same lane onto the traincar's next successor only while the unary-continuation conditions hold (a single hard successor named by the reserved traincar, other predecessors integrated or accepted-on-train, the classifier still permitting, the safety cap default 4 not reached), ending the train at a fork, join, blocker, boundary, or cap; a traincar shall be one Build pass (one commit per WI) then one review cycle over the combined diff carrying the policy-required number of independent verdicts, with no constituent done until the whole train integrates; on any early end the dispatcher shall transactionally release unstarted constituent reservations and recompute the traincar DAG; and a blocked constituent shall receive the durable disposition via the integrator while independent completed constituents still proceed.

**Acceptance criteria.** A unary chain continues on one train and stops at zero/multiple successors, a join, a blocker, a boundary, or the cap; a multi-WI traincar takes one review cycle with the policy-required verdict count and no early done; an early end (block/conflict/cap) releases unstarted reservations and recomputes the DAG; a fork integrates the parent then launches children on separate lanes; a join starts from the combined integration HEAD.

**Rationale.** Realizes SN-025 - unary dependency chains ride one branch to save integration/review overhead (the clustering trade), while fork/join and the release-on-early-end rule keep reservations from stranding (docs/specs/parallel-wi-dispatch.md 7).

# SR-062 — Change-train continuation

**Requirement.** Within an assigned traincar the dispatcher shall continue the same lane onto the traincar's next successor only while the unary-continuation conditions hold (a single hard successor named by the reserved traincar, other predecessors integrated or accepted-on-train, the classifier still permitting, the safety cap default 4 not reached), ending the train at a fork, join, blocker, boundary, or cap.

**Acceptance criteria.** A unary chain continues on one train and stops at zero or multiple successors, a join, a blocker, a boundary, or the cap; a fork integrates the parent then launches children on separate lanes; a join starts from the combined integration HEAD.

**Rationale.** Realizes SN-025 — a unary dependency chain split across lanes pays a full integration and review cycle per link for work that could not have been done in parallel anyway. Continuing without a cap was rejected: an unbounded chain concentrates the whole frontier into one un-reviewable diff and one lane's failure.

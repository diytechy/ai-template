# SR-065 — Parallel-execution telemetry and downstream migration

**Requirement.** The dispatcher shall record reason-coded telemetry (frontier decisions, reservation, worker/reviewer start/finish, continuation, fork/join, overlap, conflict, re-review, integration, recovery, quarantine, cleanup) aggregated by (train, WI, session) and surface a banner of active lanes/frontier/integration-queue/cost ceiling; and downstream migration shall be explicit - a repo runs --jobs 1 until BOTH its soft-edge audit (correctness edges promoted to hard) and its SafetyClass audit (every open WI reviewed-classified) pass, then flips to the two-worker default as a recorded promotion; legacy active rows and docs/tracks/* reconcile within one compatibility window.

**Acceptance criteria.** Telemetry emits the reason-coded events and required measurements aggregated per (train, WI, session) with no cross-session id collision; the banner reports lanes/frontier/queue/ceiling; migration keeps a repo at --jobs 1 until both audits pass and records the two-worker promotion; legacy active rows and tracks reconcile with a logged finding; a non-adopting repo is unaffected.

**Rationale.** Realizes SN-025 and SN-012 - evidence not intuition drives capacity tuning, and the opt-in flip stays safe because the two audits are what make optimistic parallelism sound; a repo that never flips pays nothing (docs/specs/parallel-wi-dispatch.md 13/14).

# SR-159 — Declared-architecture connectivity gaps are reported

**Requirement.** The delivered harness shall report declared-architecture connectivity gaps — an unresolvable interface row, an undeclared endpoint or direction, a cross-component import no declared interface covers, an invalid spec interface citation, and a top view past its declared bound — warn-first with --strict gating, vacuous where the optional inventories are absent.

**Acceptance criteria.** Malformed or unresolvable IF rows are findings; undeclared endpoints, uncovered cross-component edges, invalid spec citations and an oversized top view warn plain and fail --strict; declared opt-outs and small or absent inventories are vacuous; declared seams render without changing the verdict. The rules and their opt-outs are read off their declaration sites, as the current set: trace.py for IF/CMP id shape and the off-spine back-link to the spine (`ID_PATTERNS` and the always-on integrity floor, which joins --strict), its `REQUIRED_FIELDS["IF"]` / `["CMP"]` entries and their enums staying advisory by the recorded warn-first sequencing; check_trajectory.py for the connectivity rules -- every arch-map module resolving to at least one IF endpoint (the "connectivity undeclared" warn), a cross-component import no IF row covers, a spec's `## Interfaces` section citing an unresolvable IF-### or neither a seam nor the intra-module escape, and the `TOP_VIEW_MAX` bound of 10 items (warn plain, error under --strict); `docs/process.toml [checks] interfaces_check` and `components_check` as the two declared opt-outs, with a `source`/`sink` token in an IF row's Notes as the per-module honesty valve; and gen_arch_map.py's generated block in docs/architecture.md as the module inventory every one of those rules joins against. A connectivity rule added at one of those sites is in this row's scope by default.

**Rationale.** Realizes SN-023 (the parts' connections are as much of the truth as the requirement tree, checked mechanically warn-first — the need's own words), SN-002 (interface rows stay resolvable) and SN-012 (opt-out and vacuity keep the layer free for non-users).

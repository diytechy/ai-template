# SR-153 — Recorded, current conflict verdict per queued item

**Requirement.** The admission transaction shall record a conflict verdict of no-conflict, compatible-overlap or conflict against the spine and against every queued or active item.

**Acceptance criteria.** Overlapping candidates produce an overlap graph and an adjudicated verdict; a compatible overlap records its ordering or partition; a conflict cancels the candidate or replaces it with a draft.

**Rationale.** Mechanical overlap is a candidate finding, not a conflict: two rows touching one file may be perfectly compatible in a declared order. Recording the judgement with the digests it was computed against is what stops a stale verdict outliving the state it judged.

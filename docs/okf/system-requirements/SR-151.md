# SR-151 — An attempted work item never returns to the frontier

**Requirement.** An attempted work item shall stay terminal, with remaining scope entering the queue only as a newly identified successor carrying explicit lineage to the attempt.

**Acceptance criteria.** A terminal work item is absent from the ready frontier under every disposition; a successor drafted from a partial attempt names its predecessor and its remaining scope and enters the queue only through admission.

**Rationale.** Reviving an attempt merges two histories into one row, so the record of what was tried and rejected is overwritten by the next try. A successor keeps both records and forces the remaining scope to pass queue admission on its own merits rather than inheriting an old verdict.

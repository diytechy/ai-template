# SR-155 — Contested planning rounds for marked work

**Requirement.** For work declared as contested planning, the delivered loop content shall produce the selected decomposition through a bounded round of two independently authored rival plans — cross-critiqued, mechanically coverage-compared and arbitrated by fresh sessions — committing the selected outcome atomically as registry-valid queued rows and surfacing a non-converging round through the declared ratification level, never falling back to a single uncontested plan silently.

**Acceptance criteria.** A row marked for contested planning is refused on the direct build path; the round runs planner pair, cross-critique, mechanical coverage comparison and position-swapped arbitration as fresh sessions within declared budgets; SELECT files children that pass the registry validator atomically; PAGE preserves the round's evidence and maps through the declared ratification level.

**Rationale.** Realizes SN-024 (a decomposition judged by a competing plan and an independent arbiter rather than its author), with SN-026 (the planner pair prefers distinct families), SN-023/SN-002 (plans are compared on clause, requirement and interface coverage) and SN-006. One row because the dual-plan round is one delivered capability with one observable outcome class (SELECT or PAGE); its state machine, briefs, adapters and isolation rules are the component tier's.

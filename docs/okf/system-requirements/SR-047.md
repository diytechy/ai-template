# SR-047 — Subjective-quality critique loop

**Requirement.** When docs/agents-enabled opts in, the unattended coordinator shall schedule a fresh, provider-heterogeneous CRITIQUE session before the next build whenever a committing build's WI touches a Verification=Critique SR - the critic receiving the rubric plus the SN/SR intent plus the artifact recipe and NEVER the implementer's self-assessment - write its verdict to docs/reviews/NNN-CRITIQUE.md (a VERDICT: APPROVE|CHANGES-REQUESTED findings=N machine line plus anchor-citing findings), iterate BUILD<->CRITIQUE bounded by AGENT_CRITIQUE_MAX (default 3, env-overridable) then page the human per the docs/gate-policy failure semantics; check_trajectory.py --staged shall warn (never block) when a WI closes on a Critique SR while the latest CRITIQUE verdict is CHANGES-REQUESTED without the staged change touching the TC registry, the tests dir, or a docs/rubrics/ file; and trace.py shall accept Critique in the Verification vocabulary while keeping a Critique SR non-LLR-exempt. Absent the enable-list or any Critique SR, nothing changes.

**Acceptance criteria.** With docs/agents-enabled present and a Critique-verified SR in a committing build's WI scope, a CRITIQUE session is scheduled before the next build and not otherwise; its prompt carries the rubric plus intent and never the build transcript or self-assessment; a CHANGES-REQUESTED verdict drives a rework build and a re-critique, and AGENT_CRITIQUE_MAX rounds trip the gate-policy page-the-human path; the verdict machine line parses; the --staged ratchet warns when a Critique WI closes under a CHANGES-REQUESTED critique with no TC/tests/rubric delta and holds otherwise; trace.py accepts Critique (a Critique SR keeps its LLR) and still rejects an unknown value; and an absent enable-list or no Critique SR reproduces today's behavior.

**Rationale.** Realizes SN-024 (subjective acceptance adjudicated by an independent critical eye against a written rubric, never the authoring session) and SN-006 (the S8 chassis: fresh sessions, redacted prompts, verdict files, gate-policy escalation). Critique is a first-class Verification value (owner ruling #4) - LLM-provisioned feedback that runs autonomously, deliberately separated from human Attest; the rubric derives from the SN/SR intent not the possibly-lax TC, its numbered good/bad anchors accumulate at rework, and the ratchet keeps a lax TC from letting awkward work ship. Rides the pending G3 re-attestation.

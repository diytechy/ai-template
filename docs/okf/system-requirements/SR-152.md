# SR-152 — Hosted CI verdict is the harness's own

**Requirement.** The hosted CI job verdict shall carry the harness's own exit for the declared bar — the job failing when the harness exits nonzero, with a step log naming each step's outcome and no silently skipped required step — as the declared backstop re-run behind the bypassable local hook floor.

**Acceptance criteria.** The shipped workflow contains no continue-on-error or conditional skip around a required harness step; a nonzero harness exit fails the job; the step log names the harness invocation and its tier. This row is the backstop half of the pair SR-019 states from the local side.

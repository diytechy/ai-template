# SR-086 — Critique verification and closure ratchet

**Requirement.** trace.py shall accept Critique in Verification without exempting its LLR.

**Acceptance criteria.** Critique is accepted while unknown methods fail, and its LLR remains required.

**Rationale.** Realizes SN-024 and SN-006 — some acceptance is a judgement no assertion settles, and with no vocabulary for it those rows either claim a Test they do not have or drop off the spine.

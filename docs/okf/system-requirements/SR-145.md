# SR-145 — A lane's outcome is a claim; the adjudicator overrules by minting

**Requirement.** Every `partial` and `cancelled` close shall earn one disposition row, keyed on the close's own immutable report path; a `complete` close shall be spot-checked at a declared rate rather than gated. Continuing partial work shall mean DRAFTING A SUCCESSOR carrying explicit lineage, never re-opening a terminal row.

**Acceptance criteria.** A partial or cancelled close mints exactly one disposition; a re-sweep mints none; a genuinely second close is a second report and so a second row; a disposition row never mints a disposition for itself (refused at both ends); a successor's lineage survives the id change.

**Rationale.** The lane is entitled to assert what happened; what it MEANS is the adjudicator's, and making that authoritative by minting keeps history honest — the original move is never reversed in place. Keying the disposition on the report PATH rather than on any field of it is what makes re-sweep idempotence correct: an earlier cut keyed on the report's mutable outcome field and re-minted on every edit. Adjudicating every green close would rebuild the verdict gate under a new name, over work two gates already judged.

# SR-172 — A stalled session ends at its declared limit

**Requirement.** The delivered loop content shall end a session that has made no declared progress within its declared stall limit, reporting the stall as its own outcome rather than as success or as an ordinary failure.

**Acceptance criteria.** A session with no declared progress inside the declared stall limit ends and reports the stall as its own distinct outcome; a session making declared progress is not ended by that limit; the limit is read from a declared value; the stall outcome is distinguishable from both success and an ordinary failure at the run surface.

**Rationale.** MINTED 2026-08-17 (Sol F12, applied), the sibling of SR-171 and from the same defect: the obligation lived only in SR-026's acceptance. Realizes SN-006 and SN-025. It is a SEPARATE row from SR-171 because the two fail independently and for opposite reasons - a backoff that never gives up is the failure this row catches, and a stall limit that fires on a legitimately slow provider is the failure SR-171's bound prevents. Stated as its own outcome because a stall reported as a plain failure sends a reader looking for a defect that is not there, and reported as success is a false green.

# SR-148 — Scope frozen at claim

**Requirement.** A claimed work item's identity and scope-bearing content shall remain byte-identical between the claim commit and the terminal move.

**Acceptance criteria.** A terminal move whose spec content differs from the claim-base content is refused with the differing region named; a byte-identical move is accepted; the frozen region covers the identity, references, acceptance definition, predecessors, safety class, build tier and plan mode.

**Rationale.** A branch that can edit its own scope can also narrow it to whatever it managed to finish, which makes every completion claim unfalsifiable. Freezing the scope at claim is what lets a reviewer compare delivered work against an obligation the worker did not write.

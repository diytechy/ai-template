# SR-170 — Shared authority surfaces are the serial actor's alone

**Requirement.** The delivered loop content shall write the shared records it derives - the compiled activity log and the generated project-state artifacts - only from the serial merge step and only against the merged tree, never from a parallel work branch.

**Acceptance criteria.** The compiled activity log and the generated project-state artifacts are written only by the serial merge step and never committed from a work branch; a work branch that regenerates one is refused or its result is not what lands. Ordering and partial-result behaviour are SR-173's; identity allocation is SR-174's; the harness work-branch freshness skip is SR-006's clause and is not restated here.

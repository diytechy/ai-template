# SR-170 — Shared authority surfaces are the serial actor's alone

**Requirement.** The delivered loop content shall produce every shared authority surface — the compiled activity record, the generated project-state artifacts, and the mint of work-item identity — only at the serial integration seam and only from the composed tree, regenerating them there in declared dependency order and stopping loudly at the first failure, so no lane commits one and no two lanes can mint the same identity.

**Acceptance criteria.** The compiled activity record, the generated project-state artifacts and the work-item identity mint are written only by the serial trunk actor and never committed on a work branch — the harness's own work-branch freshness skip is SR-006's clause and is not restated here; regeneration at the seam runs in declared dependency order and stops loudly at the first failure rather than leaving a partial set; two lanes finishing against the same tree cannot mint the same work-item identity, and an identity freed by a deletion is not re-issued. Read off the current carrier, as the current set: trunk_step.py (--regen and the log compile), intake.py, integrate.py's post-merge arm, and check.py's work-branch freshness skip.

**Rationale.** Realizes SN-027's serialization guarantee at the surfaces where a parallel writer would actually corrupt something, with SN-010 (the status surface a human reads regenerates at the seam, never on a lane), SN-008 (a first failure stops loudly rather than half-regenerating) and SN-025 (the surface a session reads is generated, never hand-copied). Minted 2026-08-16 by the re-tier v2 S4 split of SR-156 (ruling R1): the parent stated the serial LANDING and, separately, that the shared authority surfaces regenerate only there — two contracts that fail independently, since a perfectly serialized landing that still lets a lane commit a regenerated artifact satisfies the landing row and not this one. The three surfaces sit in ONE row rather than three because they share one decision, not three: the serial trunk actor is their sole writer. That decision is already owner-ruled for the identity mint (log.md Decisions 2026-08-01, R1/R3 — a work branch never mints a work-item id) and this row is its requirement-tier home, which it did not have. `phase` inherits the parent's 4, matching every child.

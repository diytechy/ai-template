**Requirement.** check_flows.py shall validate that the authored runtime-flow docs conform to their contract.

**Acceptance criteria.** check_flows.py passes a conformant flow doc and fails a malformed one.

**Rationale.** Realizes SN-010 — a hand-authored flow doc that drifts from its contract is caught.

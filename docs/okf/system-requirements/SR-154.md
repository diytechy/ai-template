# SR-154 — Declared weighted job pools with capability fallback

**Requirement.** Each declared job shall resolve to a route drawn from its declared weighted pool, honouring the declared capability requirement and falling back only to a route of equal or greater strength.

**Acceptance criteria.** A job draws from its pool in declared proportion; an unavailable route falls back to an equal or stronger route; a pool with no capable route refuses rather than downgrading; the adjudicator and arbiter roles resolve through the same mechanism as the reviewer.

**Rationale.** Routing spread across a pair registry, a consent file and three launcher maps means no single place answers which model runs which role, and the adjudicator and arbiter roles had no declaration at all. Declaring pools per job puts the answer in one reviewed place and lets a fallback preserve strength instead of quietly degrading it.

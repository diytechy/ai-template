# SR-174 — Work-item identity is allocated once and never re-issued

**Requirement.** The delivered loop content shall allocate each work-item identity at most once, so that no two concurrent actors receive the same identity and an identity freed by a deletion is never re-issued.

**Acceptance criteria.** Two actors finishing against the same tree cannot obtain the same work-item identity; an identity freed by deleting its record is not re-issued to new work; allocation counts from the recorded high-water mark rather than from the live maximum.

**Rationale.** MINTED 2026-08-17 (Sol F18, applied) out of SR-170. Identity allocation is its own decision, not a consequence of serialization: serializing the writer makes a COLLISION unlikely and does nothing about RE-USE, which is the half that silently re-points every commit message and archived document citing the freed id. The non-reuse clause previously appeared only in SR-170's acceptance, which is the same acceptance-mints-a-requirement defect Sol raised at F12 - stated here rather than left in a cell that cannot carry it. Realizes SN-008 and SN-025.

# SR-142 — Red test cases under a closed implementation are named and judged

**Requirement.** The idle census shall name, as its own class, a test case that is not green while every requirement it verifies is cited by a work item recorded `done`, and shall route it to an ADJUDICATION row whose tier is estimated from measured breadth rather than defaulted.

**Acceptance criteria.** A not-green TC under a `done` citer is named; `Verified`, `Draft` and `Modified` are exempt (the last belongs to the amendment adjudication, and two judgement rows for one event is a double-count); an open, `partial` or `cancelled` citer makes nothing red; a TC verifying a mix of claimed and unclaimed targets is not red; the census line's grammar has exactly one reader.

**Rationale.** The pair is a contradiction the registries state plainly and nothing was reading: the work is closed and the evidence for it is red. It is not one situation but three — a stale TC, an optimistic close, or a moved requirement — and choosing between them is a judgement, which is what the adjudication kind is for. The statuses that are NOT red are stated as exemptions because Status is an open vocabulary: failing toward naming a gap beats failing toward missing one.

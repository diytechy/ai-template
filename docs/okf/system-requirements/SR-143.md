# SR-143 — Queue overlap is made visible, never blocked

**Requirement.** The validator shall report, warn-only and never in the exit code, pairs of OPEN work items that overlap by near-identical title, shared requirement citation, or a shared spec of record.

**Acceptance criteria.** Each overlapping OPEN pair is named once per signal with both ids, in deterministic order; a closed row never conflicts; the title bar is high enough that ordinary unrelated work is silent and a shared stopword is not a signal; the findings never join the exit code, not even under --strict.

**Rationale.** A queue that silently grows two rows for one job is the failure; a checker that cannot tell a legitimate split from a duplicate must not block on the difference. Overlap is frequently correct — two rows may answer one requirement from different sides — so the whole contribution of the mechanical rung is visibility, and the judgement half belongs to an adjudicator with the scope in front of it.

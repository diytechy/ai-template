## 4. Counterexamples the review should try

1. Put a need under a plausible but irrelevant prose objective. Can the purpose
   review detect the semantic mismatch even though every ordinary link resolves?
   No objective-field validator is commissioned.
2. Upgrade an adopter with a different vision and customized hats. Can its review
   identify a missing domain lens, propose a genuinely missing SN and rederive
   the affected SRs without copying kit promises or overwriting its approvals?
3. Resume a provider conversation twice with cumulative counters; replay the
   second result. Does usage appear exactly once per invocation? Include an
   auxiliary model whose cost is already in the parent total.
4. Kill a provider before it reports usage, then kill the coordinator after
   receiving a result but before export. Are unknown spend and metric recovery
   honest, and does missing telemetry avoid becoming fabricated approval?
5. Return material findings, minor suggestions and a disputed criterion in one
   review. Does the fresh builder receive correct scope and previous decisions
   without treating every suggestion as mandatory work?
6. Exercise WI-551 retention alongside fresh builders. Can an adjudicator keep
   permitted context without becoming the independent reviewer of its own work?
7. Reconcile WI-557 with acceptance receipts. Can the owner still edit the
   promised review notes without rewriting an authoritative acceptance receipt?
8. Compose a candidate that changes its own approval policy; tighten trunk
   policy while its build is running. Which revision authorizes each act?
9. Amend design while tests for new behavior are intentionally failing, and
   separately introduce a regression in already-delivered behavior. Are the
   two cases distinguishable without weakening current mandatory bars?
10. Migrate approval snapshots while WI-596's absorbed-row concern is present.
    Can unrelated changes accidentally be blessed by copying a whole file?
11. Rework three times and add slow intake judgment under two lanes. Does P5
    count all exclusive time and demonstrate simplicity after adding receipts,
    refs, local metric flushing and recovery procedures?
12. Stop after the first accepted new-runner completion and roll back. Are new
    accepted work, open claims, old judgments and retained patches preserved
    without selecting two mutating runners or resetting away history?

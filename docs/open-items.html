SR-208Held-rung changes are refused before they landre-accept owed

Requirement

Where the declared acceptance level holds a snapshotted registry's rung for an operator, the delivered harness shall refuse, before it lands on trunk, both a lane's acceptance claim that is not a CLARITY re-acceptance bound to the row's judged text and a marked autonomous commit outside a lane that moves a held status or makes a held acceptance claim.

Rationale

The acceptance level names the rungs an operator still accepts, and an automated acceptance of the spine's own rows is already refused. The frame, assumption, surrogate and component registries carry status cells of their own that no automated path was stopped from changing, because the one setting that would stop it defaults to allowing. A policy that declares a rung operator-held without refusing the automated path is a claim, not a control. A lane can carry an acceptance into trunk even when no automated writer made its commit, and an autonomous commit outside a lane can make the same change; protecting only selected writers leaves the held state dependent on who delivered it. Binding the permitted re-acceptance to its judged text prevents a verdict about one text authorizing another, while leaving an unmarked reviewed trunk acceptance to its operator authority.

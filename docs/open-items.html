Rationale

Without one precedence rule, the same repository can resume into implementation while returned obligations or prerequisite requirement tiers remain unresolved. SR-238A deadline or interruption ends the session process treere-accept owed

Requirement

When a declared deadline or coordinator interruption ends a session, the delivered coordinator shall end every process in the session process group on POSIX or session job on Windows.

Rationale

A descendant can outlive the direct session child and keep editing the worktree handed to the next session. Ending only the direct child was rejected because it leaves that concurrent writer outside the coordinator's supervision. The bounded guarantee excludes a descendant that leaves the session process group and a process another service starts outside the session job: neither is ended, and the kill does not report it. A POSIX zombie under a PID 1 that never reaps keeps the group nonempty, so the kill raises after the bound. A descendant that denies the kill access to wait on it also makes the kill raise, rather than letting the coordinator continue without knowing whether it ended. Stopping is the need's cannot-proceed case, not a supervision fault: continuing would hand the worktree to the next session beside a writer that may still be live, and nothing can proceed safely past that. Two windows lie outside the guarantee, each sub-second and on no supported workflow's ordinary path: an interruption while the session is still being launched, before the coordinator supervises it, which can leave a running child; and a process that joins the session job while the kill takes its member list, which is terminated with the job but can still be ending, for milliseconds, when the coordinator continues.

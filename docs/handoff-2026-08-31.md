### 1.5 The recovery path to intake, step by step

1. **The branch is already backed up** — pushed to `origin` on 2026-08-31, after
   this document's first draft said it was single-copy. Nothing below risks it.
2. **Rename the ref back** to `wi508-architectural-remap`, so it matches the
   claim directory on trunk. The lane is then a *finished* branch.
3. **The refresh will refuse** on the six conflicts of §1.4 — the same refusal
   shape as `integrate: refresh REFUSED ... merging trunk ... CONFLICTS`.
   Resolve on the branch (take trunk's generated side; splice `log.md`), commit,
   then let the slot redo its own refresh.
4. **The verdict rung will then refuse anyway**, and here is the arithmetic it
   uses — worth reading closely, because it is the whole subject of the plan:

   ```
   compiled rollup, last commit : 2026-08-30 05:48:44  (a2b8d087, governing "VERDICT: APPROVE findings=0", round 010)
   last NON-record commit       : 2026-08-30 08:50:42  (6176166d, the brief regeneration)
   ```

   The gate compares those two and refuses: *"predates the branch's last code
   commit — a stale APPROVE does not clear the gate."* Re-compiling the rollup
   honestly from all thirteen rounds makes it worse, not better: the governing
   round is then 019, which is `CHANGES-REQUESTED`.
5. **So exactly two things can make this lane merge:** a fresh reviewer round
   that approves the refreshed tree, or a human writing a fresh rollup that says
   `APPROVE`. **The gate cannot tell those apart** — nothing in the kit verifies
   a rollup against the rounds it claims to summarize, and this session authored
   four of them by hand last night. That is not an argument against doing it; it
   is the reason `OI-70` and §1 of the plan exist, and you are looking at the
   cleanest live example of it.
6. **Before spending a round, fix the two renderer defects (§1.2)** — they are
   trunk-side and caused two of the three blocking findings, so a round drawn
   before they are fixed is likely to re-find them.

### 1.6 The one thing I could not settle — and a trap that will bite you too

The branch rewrites `docs/archive/last_approved/` (an 1822-line churn in its
`interfaces.toml` copy). Round 010 raised this as a BLOCKER — "the rework
hand-edited the `last_approved` snapshot" — and decisions 8 and 9 record it as
*flagged, not undone* and *confirmed in part, refuted in part on driven
evidence*. **It is still open, and it is the highest-risk thing in the lane:**
that directory is the anchor the whole re-attestation model measures drift
against, and it is supposed to be provably a COPY of a live registry made in an
approval commit, never authored text.

**The trap:** I tried to test that invariant by checking whether the branch's
snapshot blob ever existed as a live `docs/requirements/interfaces.toml`. It
never did — which looks damning until you run the control: **trunk's own
current snapshot fails the identical test**, and so does every snapshot file I
checked on both sides. So blob equality is *not* the invariant, and a fresh
session that runs my first test alone will "confirm" a defect that may not
exist. Use the kit's own comparison instead (`baseline_snapshot`,
`trace.py --approve modified`) and establish what the snapshot writer actually
guarantees before drawing any conclusion.

## 5. Operating facts worth knowing before touching the loop

- The full suite is **~11 minutes** (3192 passed / 15 skipped / 643.9 s at
  `ada265fd`); a worker's tool turn caps at 10, so a close that requires the
  full suite **cannot complete in one turn** — three such sessions is the stall
  guard, and it closed a finished row `partial` on 2026-08-31.
- The loop's stdout is block-buffered when redirected; watch the lane's
  `git log` and `out/run-logs/` rather than the log file.
- The dispatcher drains only before an **exclusive** claim or at station exit, so
  a lane you finish by hand between runs waits — merge it yourself from the trunk
  root (`integrate.py --root <trunk> integrate`).
- **Never touch a tracked trunk file while the loop runs**: the slot refuses a
  dirty trunk and the run stops (it happened, and it was my error).
- After merging a lane that regenerated `docs/ratify/CURRENT.md`, regenerate it
  again **on trunk** — its provenance line is history-derived and goes stale on
  merge, redding the *next* lane's refresh.
- The OpenAI usage limit on this account resets ~08:40 UTC and is shared across
  SOL/TERRA/LUNA; when both cross-family providers are down, the reviewer rung
  falls back to an independent Opus with heterogeneity **relaxed and recorded**.

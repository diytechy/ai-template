## 5. WI-322 in detail (the part most likely to bite you)

`docs/open-items.md` is retired. `docs/requirements/open-items.csv` is the source
and `gen_open_items.py` renders it — plus **every `Draft`/`Modified` spine row's
per-cell word-level before/after** — into `docs/open-items.html`. New `open-items`
step in `check.py` and in the pre-commit floor.

**Invariants you must not break:**

- **One computation, two renderers.** `trace.reattest_model` owns the git
  archaeology and the cell comparison; `reattest_lines` renders markdown, the
  view renders HTML. `gen_open_items` also imports `gen_trajectory.pending_block`
  rather than re-deriving what is pending. If the view and `trace.py --ratify`
  ever disagree, **the brief is authoritative and the view is the bug**.
- **The view self-stamps its baseline** and *reuses it* when a later run passes
  no `--since`. This is not a nicety: without it the unattended loop's
  regeneration silently collapsed 43 chain-row diffs to 18 and `--check`
  certified the loss. `--since ""` is the explicit opt-back-to-auto.
- **An empty attestation section means CHECK THE BASELINE**, never "nothing
  changed". A pre-regime amendment (one that landed while the row still read
  `Verified`) sits *before* the auto-derived baseline.
- **OI ids have been reused historically** (`OI-8`, `OI-9` each name two
  different decisions). Ruled history is therefore **not** backfilled into the
  registry — pending rows plus forward tombstones; the Decisions log is the
  record.

**The adversarial review ([122-REVIEW-A](reviews/122-REVIEW-A.md))** returned
CHANGES-REQUESTED findings=10; all are fixed and nine guards are mutation-proven.
Two lessons generalize beyond this WI:

1. **A guard that reads a constant instead of the artifact tests your intention,
   not your output.** My theme "drift guard" compared against a dict nothing
   rendered from — nine of twelve emitted tokens were rewritten and it stayed
   green. Second occurrence in two days (WI-293 was the first).
2. **Two fixes for one defect can mask each other.** The CRLF bug needed three
   seams (strip at source, LF-only write, normalized compare); with all three in,
   *no* single mutation turned any test red until I split the test so each
   isolates one fix.

## 6. Traps this session paid for

- **The commit bar runs the WARN tier.** A module can land in the arch map, in no
  component, with an undeclared cross-component import, and the pre-commit floor
  stays green. Run `check.py --gate <gate>` before closing any module-adding WI.
- **Never `--no-verify`.** The floor caught three of my own errors in one
  session (a malformed CSV row, IF rows declared-but-unfiled, a stale dashboard).
- **`docs/pause` cannot stop a running worker** — it is a *dispatcher*-scope
  control. Only killing the worker stops one.
- **Check a branch for orphaned FILES before deleting it**, not just for merged
  work — the last cleared train held two `docs/iteration/` logs that existed
  nowhere else.
- **A `Modified` section with no cells is a signal to check the baseline**, never
  a row that is free to bless. A bad `--since` fabricates a brief; no `--since`
  empties one. Both fail silently in front of a human about to attest.
- **`refs/llm/` is empty and the 2026-07-26 train worktree is cleared.** Older

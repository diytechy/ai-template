## Done-when

lands (transient working state — this spec archives at close).

- [ ] The spine reflects it (the delivered `SR-###` and its `LLR/TC` chain).
- [ ] `status.md` names the next work; this WI's row is `done` with its
      `Deliverable` filled and `SpecRef` cleared.
